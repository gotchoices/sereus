# Strand reactivity: real-network scenario — review handoff

## What was built

**Harness (additive, defaults unchanged).** `TopologyMachineSpec` and `ControlNodeOpts` gain `strandClusterSize` and `strandReactivity`, passed straight into `CadreNodeConfig` the way `revocationPollMs` is. `StrandJoinSpec` gains `clusterSize`, which caps `joinStrandOn`'s cohort barrier at `min(members, clusterSize)`. Without it, a strand at breadth 2 with three members waits for a 3-wide cohort that can never form.

**Scenario** `strand-reactivity-wakes-watchers.integration.ts`: one party, three machines (production profiles), one strand at `strandClusterSize: 2`, `strandReactivity: { enabled: true }` everywhere. The sApp declares one table with `with tags ("optimystic.network_watch" = true)`. In order:

1. Assert that the schema tag opened a network watch on every machine (`reactivityWatch.watchedCount === 1`). This is the production path from sApp tag to plugin.
2. Turn the tag off everywhere, commit a seed row, then turn the tag back on one machine at a time. Wait for each machine's registration (`reactivityWatch.isAttached`), then 1 s, then the next machine. After the last one, wait 10 s (two cohort gossip intervals). Both waits are workarounds; their `NOTE:`s explain them, and "Deviations" below says why.
3. Only then open each machine's app-level `db.watch` (one `select` first). A tag change is a schema change, which ends every Quereus watch on the table, and the watch service's first-read wake has already fired by now.
4. Wrap each strand node's untyped `reactivitySubscribers.deliver` to timestamp notifications for the watched collection, then call through. The test fails with a clear message if the attachment is missing.
5. Three rounds; in round *i* machine *i* commits. Per watcher, assert: woken within 35 s of the commit resolving; and a `deliver` occurred, followed by a wake ("pushed"). Name the tail's root group from the delivered notification (`keyNetwork.servingCohortAt(reactivityRootCoord(tailId))`), and assert that some watcher was outside it. Without that check, a breadth setting that silently failed to apply would make the outsider claim vacuous. Each round prints one summary line with commit-to-wake and commit-to-deliver latencies.

## Results (Optimystic 1.10.0 linked, loopback, one Windows machine)

- **The machine outside the tail's storage group is woken by push.** Final form: 7 of 7 runs pass. In every run the outsider was a watcher in two rounds and was pushed in both. Its wake came 52 ms before to 53 ms after the committing insert returned; all watchers ranged from 83 ms before to 53 ms after. Inserts took 70–148 ms. Deliveries often arrive before the committer's `exec` resolves. Registration took 1.5–41 s for the first machine at a root (proof of work, plus a 30 s retry if the first attempt backs off) and 52–68 ms for each later one. A run takes 10–46 s of test time plus about 11 s of imports.
- **The plugin README's "every machine in every cohort" caveat is out of date.** That is reported in `blocked/report-optimystic-reactivity-outside-tail-group`, and docs/strands.md now says a strand above its breadth gets push wakes. The ticket's "all-storage rerun" was conditional on the outsider failing, so it was not needed.
- **Optimystic defect: registrations that arrive together lock later ones out.** With the tag left on from the schema, every machine registers on the same renewal tick after the first commit. In 5 of 8 runs one machine never registered: `CohortBackoffError: no willing primary right now` every 30 s, and a non-root engine logging "gathered 3 of 14 required signatures". With a 0 s gap between registrations the third machine failed in 2 of 2 runs; with 1 s it never failed (20 of 20). The mechanism is read from the code, not instrumented: slope pre-promotion at the tier-0 root (`promotion.ts` `slopePredictsCrossing`), no demotion at a root, and a tier-1 cohort that needs 14 signatures. In failing runs, a registered machine also stopped being pushed about 90 s after registering, and once the unregistered machine's 30 s tail reads stopped too. Reported in `blocked/report-optimystic-reactivity-registration-burst-on-a-small-strand`; recorded in docs/strands.md → "Registrations that arrive together".
- **A few seconds after registering, a commit can reach nobody.** When round 1 began about 1 s after the last registration, 3 of 13 runs had round 1 pushed to no one. A temporary probe on `cohortTopicHost.service.onLocalCommit` showed one root member announcing the commit, yet no `deliver` was called anywhere. The likely cause is that registrations reach the other root member only through 5 s cohort gossip. With the 10 s wait: 0 of 7. This is in the same upstream report and in docs/strands.md → "What a watcher sees".

## Deviations from the ticket, for the reviewer to judge

- **Tag toggling and staggered registration instead of "the schema tag registers on its own".** The natural flow fails about 60% of the time because of the upstream defect. The ticket's prescribed fallback was to weaken the push assertion to "at least one watcher per round". In the failing runs, though, even the per-watcher 30 s bound failed (run 6) and registered insiders lost pushes (run 5), so no assertion that held in every observed run would still prove the outsider claim. Instead the scenario avoids the trigger, keeps the per-watcher push and bound assertions unconditional, and names the upstream report in the `NOTE:` on `REGISTRATION_SPACING_MS`, which says to remove the workaround once Optimystic is fixed. The cost: CI does not exercise the natural registration flow, and a regression in it would go unseen.
- **The first wake is not "waited for and discarded"** as the ticket describes. It fires before registration lands, and app watches open only after registration, so round 1 cannot count it.
- **`RECORD_GOSSIP_SETTLE_MS` is a fixed 10 s sleep**, sized from the 5 s gossip interval. The cause behind it is a hypothesis (consistent with the probe, not proven). 7 clean runs cannot rule out a flake rate below about 1 in 7.

## Known gaps

- The scenario reaches into Optimystic internals: the untyped `reactivitySubscribers` attachment, `reactivityWatch.isAttached` / `watchedCount`, and a collection id derived from `defaultCollectionUri('app', 'Data')` with `tree://` removed (the plugin's private `parseCollectionId`; the schema name is Quereus's lowercase canonical form). An Optimystic rename fails loudly: the registration wait times out with the registered state in the message.
- Waking the outsider is attributed to the push by a `deliver` call followed by a wake. A machine outside the tail group can still store other blocks of the commit and wake locally, and some rounds show that (a wake before the deliver). The assertion needs a wake after the deliver, which a local wake that came first does not satisfy.
- Not measured: a strand where some of the tail's storage machines lack the option (docs still say so), tail rotation, and edge-profile hosts.
- `alter table … set tags` on a strand is local and not persisted (plugin README). The scenario relies on that, and only within a run.
