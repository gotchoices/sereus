# Invitations redeemable by any member: the old path is gone, every caller moved

## What changed

**cadre-core.** `SeedBootstrapService.createInvite`, `acceptPhone`, `encodeInvite`, `decodeInvite`, `dialInvite` and their `CadreNode` wrappers are deleted, with the types `CadreInvite`, `AddPhoneOptions`, `InviteResult`, the service's `inviteAddressResolver` config field (its one reader was `createInvite`; `CadreNodeConfig.network.inviteAddressResolver` and `setInviteAddresses` stay, read by `resolveInviteAddresses` for the cadre invitation's own address), `CadreNode.openEnrollmentWindow`, the `enrollmentWindowUntil` field, `DEFAULT_ENROLLMENT_WINDOW_MS` and the window check in `admitInboundControlConnection`. The gate's checks are renumbered (old 3 to 7 are now 2 to 6; the blocked ticket `decide-cadre-invite-redeemed-before-the-row-replicates` names check 6 now). Doc comments that said "carried by `CadreInvite.ownerKeys`" say the cadre invitation's `ownerKeys`. `addDrone`, `addPhoneWithRelay`, `authorizePeer`, `deliverSeed` and `applySeed` stay: the owner-online paths.

**cadre-cli.** The admin channel loses `POST /admin/accept-phone`; `POST /admin/invites` takes `{ peerId?, grantsOwner?, expiresInMs?, uses? }` (each field shape-checked; a bad one is `400 bad_request` naming it) and returns `{ invitation, encoded }`; `GET /admin/invites` lists every invitation the node holds with its standing; `DELETE /admin/invites/:key` withdraws and answers `{ withdrawn }`. `cadre enroll invite [--peer-id <id>] [--owner] [--ttl <duration>] [--uses <n>]` prints the encoded bundle on stdout and the report on stderr, with a warning when `--owner` has no `--peer-id` (a bearer credential for admin rights, 15-minute default). `cadre start --invitation <encoded>` decodes and party-checks before anything starts, redeems right after `node.start()`, logs the accepting member, and keeps running on a failure as a failed `--seed` does. `--invitation` refuses `--seed` and `--owner` (see "Decisions"). The admin-connection option parsing `enroll add` owned (`--admin-port`, `--token-file`, `--timeout`, the operator-facing failure text) moved into `admin-client.ts` so both enroll commands share it. `enroll create`'s next-steps text names both routes.

**cadre-host.** The trust circle is deleted outright: `src/auth/` (service, store, handlers, self-label, their tests; `duration.ts` and its test moved to `src/donation/`, still used by `grant issue --ttl`), `routes/trust-circle.ts` and its mount, the `/auth/*` rows in `static.ts`, `OwnerNodeClient.createInvite`/`acceptPhone`/`encodeInvite`, the `invite` and `trust` CLI commands and `--no-invite`, the installer's first-invite step, the `TrustCircle` UI page, `InviteModal.svelte`, the `trust-circle` route and state, the `trust-circle-changed` event, `trustCircle` on `/api/status`, and the `founder.trustCircle` wiring (`FounderServices` is `{ nat, strands }`). In integration-tests, `test-cadre-host.ts` drops the trust-circle fakes, `cadre-host-trust-circle.integration.ts` is deleted, the two SSE trust-circle tests are removed (the harness wires no grant service to drive a replacement event), `cadre-host-bootstrap` drops the trust-circle status assertion, and `cadre-host-owner-node`'s add-then-remove cycle admits through `addDrone`; its push-addresses test only asserts the push is accepted, since the addresses are observable only in a minted invitation (`cadre-core/test/invite-address-push.spec.ts` pins that).

**Reference apps.** The "Paste enrollment invite (for trust)" input, `ownerKeysFromInvite` and `applySeed`'s pin argument are gone in both apps; the seed field stays with a hint that a seed only works when the phone already trusts its signer. ## Decisions a reviewer should weigh

- **`--invitation` refuses `--owner`.** `--owner` runs the founder's genesis `OwnerKey` insert on a fresh store; on a node joining someone else's cadre that would seat a second founding key beside theirs before the real rows replicate in. An invitation that grants ownership seats this node's key by consent, and the node wires it for signing on a later start with `--owner` and without `--invitation`. The ticket only required refusing `--seed`; the plan ticket `cadre-host-join-a-cadre` may want a one-start path (redeem, then wire the key without genesis), a bounded follow-up in `start.ts`.
- **A failed redemption at start is logged, not fatal**, mirroring `--seed`, and **nothing retries a retryable refusal**: not `cadre start --invitation`, not the apps. The new scenario shows a member answering retryably for a window after the owner leaves (see "The scenario"); a headless machine started inside that window stays un-admitted until the operator re-runs. The app plan tickets (`app-joins-existing-cadre-by-invitation`, `reference-apps-join-existing-cadre`) own progress and retry states; the CLI may want a bounded retry too.
- **The new scenario deviates from the ticket's shape, with measurements**: the owner listens on a port (not phone-shaped) and there are two always-on members. Both deviations are the two fix tickets filed below; the file header says why.
- **The withdrawal arm of the new scenario relies on the members' relay server** (the storage-profile default): with no live invitation held, a member's gate admits Q's connection for relay only and drops it at the not-reserving deadline, which is long enough for the exchange. With the relay off, Q would see `CadreInviteUnreachableError`. The `enrollment-e2e` expired case avoids this: the owner alone has no authorized member, so its gate admits everyone.
- **The gater and stream-gate unit specs lost their window cases** rather than gaining replacements: live-invitation admission was already pinned (`admits a stranger while a live cadre invitation exists`); the stream spec's window case was rewritten against an injected live `CadreInvite` row (connection admitted, stream still refused).
- **`invite-address-push.spec.ts` now drives `createCadreInvitation`** with the control database, the sibling lookup and the anchor stubbed; its last case changed meaning: an empty pushed list makes the mint refuse ("no address a device could dial") instead of minting an invite with no addresses.
- **`ed25519-key.spec.ts`'s anchored-keys block** keeps the one branch the new mint still has (a replicated-only `OwnerKey` never rides out as a pin); its other two cases are pinned by `cadre-node-cadre-invitation.spec.ts` or changed meaning (an empty anchor now refuses to mint).
- **In the apps, the seed field stays** (demoted, with the hint), since a founding phone and a phone that redeemed an owner's invitation still apply later seeds from that owner.

## The scenario that proves the feature

`packages/integration-tests/src/scenarios/cadre-invite-any-member.integration.ts`, one test, 8-minute budget, 13 to 33 s measured: owner A (listening on loopback WebSocket; its control storage and bootstrap-peer store survive a restart) founds the cadre and admits always-on members M and N (storage profile, listening, pinning A's key as an operator would) through `addDrone` plus `deliverSeed` plus a reconcile pass; waits until their signed address records reach A; mints an untargeted owner-granting invitation (asserts the bundle names A first, then both members, and carries A's key); waits until both hold the live row; stops A; waits until A has left both members' FRET cohorts (4 to 5 s measured, logged). Phone-shaped device P (no pinned key) redeems with a bounded retry on `CadreInviteUnreachableError` (admitted on attempt 1 in 3 of 3 runs): the result names a member and `grantsOwner`; P pinned A's key; the admitting member lists P as authorized and holds P's `OwnerKey` row; the other member lists P by replication; P lists the admitting member once rows replicate and holds its outbound connection to it. A restarts over its kept stores, re-wires its signing key, reconnects and lists P. A withdraws; once both members hold the tombstone, device Q is refused with `CadreInviteRejectedError` code `invite-spent` and is not a member anywhere.

Not added to `docs/testing.md`'s topology coverage map: that map lists network shapes, and this scenario's shape (a three-machine control plane) is already covered; its subject is the protocol.

**Two findings from getting it to pass, each filed to `fix/`:**

- `phone-owner-never-leaves-members-cohort`: with A phone-shaped (`listenAddrs: []`), A never left M's cohort within 300 s, alone or with N, so every control write at the members waited on A for the whole commit budget and failed (the device timed out at 28.5 s; the member's write failed at about 50 s and answered `conflict`). With a listen address A was evicted in about 5 s. FRET's dead verdict needs failed contacts, and an address-less peer cannot be contacted. This is the household shape the feature is for; the fix may be a sibling change.
- `redemption-write-tears-on-a-member-whose-cohort-just-shrank`: with A listening and M the only member, M's first redemption write after A left committed `CadreInviteUsage` but not `CadrePeer`/`OwnerKey` (`CoordinatorPartialCommitError`, a torn action on `CadrePeer`); P's retry 22 s later was admitted, M listed P, but the restarted A never listed P within 60 s. Seen once; with two members the write committed cleanly in 3 of 3 runs.
