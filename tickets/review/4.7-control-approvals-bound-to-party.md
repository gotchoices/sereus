# Bind every control approval to its party: review handoff

## What changed

**Field vector.** `controlAuthorizationFields(domain, action, partyId, rowFields)` returns `[domain, action, partyId, ...rowFields]`. `buildAuthorizationMessage` (control-database.ts) and the private `taggedDigest` (peer-authorization.ts) take the same required positional `partyId`. A new `unboundAuthorizationFields(domain, action, rowFields)` serves the two digests that carry no party; its `domain` parameter is typed `UnboundControlDomain` (`'CadreControl.FormationUsage' | 'Cadre.Enrollment'`) and the bound builders take `BoundControlDomain` (everything else), so the type checker refuses a bound digest for `FormationUsage` and an unbound one for any other table. Both types and the unbound builder are exported from the package index.

**SQL.** Every `digest('CadreControl.<Table>', '<action>', ...)` in both schema copies now has `party_id()` third, 28 digests per copy; the two `FormationUsage` digests (`'vouch'` and `'consent'`) are the only ones left alone, with the reason written once at `FormationUsage.Authorized` and a pointer at `PeerConsented`. The header comment states the rule, and the sentence in `OwnerKey.Authorized` about two parties sharing an owner key is gone. The two copies are byte-identical (the drift guard still passes by inspection: the same regex rewrite was applied to both, and the structural guard below reads the embedded copy).

**`party_id()`.** `registerPartyIdFunction(db, partyId)` in control-database.ts registers a zero-argument, deterministic, synchronous scalar on a Quereus `Database` returning the configured party id. `ControlDatabase.initialize` calls it right after the optimystic plugin registration and before `hydrate`, since a warm restart parses the persisted CHECK text. The `ControlDatabase` constructor throws on an empty or non-string `partyId`.

**TypeScript signers and verifiers.** `partyId` is the first parameter of every bound builder and verifier in peer-authorization.ts, of `peerRecordSignedPayload` / `signPeerRecord` / `verifyPeerRecordSignature`, and of `deviceTokenSignedPayload` / `signDeviceTokenRecord` / `verifyDeviceTokenSignature`; `cadreInviteAddMessage`, `cadreInviteRedeemMessage` and `cadreInviteConsentMessage` take it first too. `OwnerAnchorInputs` gained `partyId`, threaded through the anchor rule's three private helpers. Every in-class `ControlDatabase` signer passes `this.config.partyId`; `cadre-node.ts` passes `this.partyId` at its seven sites; `seed-bootstrap.ts`'s own device-token insert passes `this.config.partyId`; the invite protocol signs and verifies the reply with `invitation.partyId` (after the existing party comparison) and the member verifies the request with `this.options.partyId` (after its existing party check). `formationVouchMessage`, `formationConsentMessage`, `formationConsentDigest`, `verifyFormationConsent`, `peerAuthorizationDigest` and `verifyPeerAuthorization` are unchanged in shape and now build through the unbound helpers.

**Docs.** docs/architecture.md gained one paragraph after the control table list stating the binding, where the SQL reads the party from, the `FormationUsage` exception, and which specs pin it; the `'redeem'` / `'consent'` lines of the redemption request shape say the digests are bound to the party.

**Test helpers.** `revocationMessage(partyId, ...)` and `reissueMessage(partyId, ...)` in control-constraint-helpers.ts; `vouchedRow(partyId, peerId, owner)` in membership-gate-helpers.ts; `insertResolvableOfflinePeer` in control-db-node-helpers.ts signs with the node's party. Every spec and integration scenario the type checker flagged, plus the three gate specs that consume `vouchedRow` (not flagged, but they would have failed at runtime), pass the node's own `CadreNode.partyId`, or one module constant where no node is booted. Deliberately-refused signatures in existing specs keep the correct party, so each refusal still pins its stated cause.

**Lint.** `control-party-binding.spec.ts` is added to the `no-restricted-syntax` exemption list for raw `CadrePeer` writes, with its reason: it presents a voucher signed for another party, which `insertCadrePeer` cannot produce (it builds the message for its own party and mints the stamp internally).

## Tests added

Catches a future digest that forgets the binding. No node boot.
