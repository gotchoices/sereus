# The unclaimed gate in `CadreNode`, and `claimNode` on the owner side

Second of three tickets. `node-claim-proof-and-policy` landed the proof and the policy below `CadreNode`; this ticket wired them into the node. The cadre-cli surface and the end-to-end scenario are `node-claim-cli-and-scenario`.

## What shipped

- **Configuration.** `CadreNodeConfig.claim?: { secret: string }` (`types.ts`). `start()` calls a new `initializeClaimPolicy()` right after `initializeTrustedOwnerStore()`, before any network bring-up: `claim` beside a non-empty `trustedOwners.pinnedKeys` or a `seedTrustPolicy` throws naming the conflict, and a malformed secret throws through `parseClaimSecret` (which never echoes the value). The policy is built once per node instance over the node's anchor, with `onClaimed` emitting the new `claim:accepted` event, and kept across `stop()`→`start()` like the anchor it writes. `initializeSeedBootstrap` throws on a node with `claim` set.
- **One policy accessor.** `inboundSeedTrustPolicy()` returns the claim policy when configured, else `config.seedTrustPolicy`; the owner service, the listener and `applySeed`'s temp service all read it. A node with `claim` calls `enableSeedListener()` itself inside `start()`.
- **`isAwaitingClaim()`** (public): `config.claim !== undefined && anchor.size === 0`, read live from the anchor. False on a node with no claim secret, on a claimed one, and before `start()` has built the anchor.
- **The unclaimed gate.** `authorizeInboundControlStream` and `admitControlRelayReservation` refuse everyone as their first check while `isAwaitingClaim()`, ahead of the empty-anchor and bootstrap-infra admissions. `admitInboundControlConnection` is unchanged: the empty-anchor rule already admits the connection the claim seed rides. After the claim the anchor is non-empty and the existing rules apply (empty authorized snapshot admits the claimant's streams, the replication cold start).
- **`claimNode({ peerId, multiaddrs, secret })`.** Requires the owner-keyed seed service (same error as `addDrone`). Parses the secret, mints a seed, builds the proof over `seedDigest(seed)`, delivers with the object target and `claimProof`. A refusal throws `ClaimRefusedError` (new, in `claim-proof.ts`, exported from the package root; carries `nodePeerId`, `reason` and the node's `code` when given) and writes nothing. On acceptance: `authorizePeer(peerId, multiaddrs)`, `retainDialTarget(..., 'claimNode')`, then an un-awaited `reconcileControlCohort()` kick with its rejection logged. Idempotent for the same owner, as the doc comment states.
- **Events.** `CadreNodeEvents['claim:accepted']: { ownerKey }`, documented as preceding that seed's `seed:applied`.
- **Docs.** `membership-connection-gater.ts` module doc (seed entry of the stranger allowlist), `docs/architecture.md` ("What it admits anyway" and "Per-stream control-DB gate"), `docs/api.md` (`claimNode`, `isAwaitingClaim`).

## Deviations from the ticket, for the reviewer

- `enableSeedListener()` runs after the cadre-invite handler registration and **before** `control:connected`, not at the literal end of `start()`: an embedder that reports "awaiting claim" on that event is then already reachable on the seed protocol. A listener registration failure fails `start()` on the same cleaned-up path as every other bring-up failure.
- `ClaimRefusedError` lives in `claim-proof.ts` rather than `cadre-node.ts` (which is near 9,000 lines and has a size-debt ticket), and carries `nodePeerId` in addition to the `code` and `reason` the ticket named.
- The claim policy is constructed once per node instance (first `start()`), not once per `start()`. Consequence: its in-process latch and failure window survive an in-process restart. Harmless; after a claim the anchor answers before either is consulted.

## Tests

- `test/cadre-node-claim.spec.ts`, one test: on a running-looking node with a claim secret and an empty anchor, a stranger's connection is admitted, its control-database stream and relay reservation are refused, and `isAwaitingClaim()` is true; after `anchor.trust(key, 'claim')` the predicate is false and both the stream and the reservation are admitted (the cold-start admissions). A control node with the same empty anchor and no claim secret admits both, pinning that the refusal is keyed on the claim rather than on the empty anchor.
- Nothing added for the configuration conflicts, the `initializeSeedBootstrap` refusal, or the local `applySeed` refusal on an unclaimed node (`claim-proof-invalid`, since a local apply carries no proof): each is one assertion at a seam or follows by inspection from the policy's decision order.
- **No unit test for `claimNode`.** Its behaviour (refusal leaves no row and no dial target; acceptance vouches and retains; idempotent re-claim) is assigned by the ticket to the scenario in `node-claim-cli-and-scenario`. The refused-writes-nothing property rests on the method's ordering (seed first, row second), which the doc comment states and the scenario's step 2 asserts.

## Known gaps and things to look at

- **Idle stranger connections on an unclaimed node.** The connection gate answers `'admit'` (not `'admit-for-relay'`) for any empty-anchor node, so the gater's drop-if-not-reserving deadline does not apply: a stranger can hold an idle connection on an unclaimed node indefinitely, speaking nothing. This is the pre-existing shape for every un-enrolled node and the ticket chose "connections unchanged"; it is worth a reviewer's eye now that such a node may sit on a public address waiting.
- `isAwaitingClaim()` is false before `start()` (no anchor yet); a caller that reads it before start sees "not awaiting". cadre-cli reads it after start.
- Restart cases (claimed node restarted with the secret still set; secret changed after the claim) are by inspection: the anchor is non-empty, so the policy answers on its anchored branch and the gate is open.
