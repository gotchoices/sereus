# Provider join scenarios with a phone-shaped requester (implemented)

Two new integration scenarios run the claim and invitation paths of `provider-container-joins-by-claim-or-invitation` against the real provider and a real `cadre-cli` child. Both pass (8 and 6 steps, about 23 seconds of wall clock each, all children included). Typecheck and the repo-wide lint pass. Nothing in a product package changed.

## What was built

**`provider-container-claimed-by-phone.integration.ts`.** The provider is `createProviderServer` over `MemoryStore` and `ProviderProcessOrchestrator` (`publicHost: 'localhost'`, `adminChannel`), driven in-process with Fastify's `inject` in auth mode `none`; the requester is `buildPhoneOwner`. Steps: requester up listening on nothing; `POST /containers { join: { kind: 'claim' } }` answers the placeholder party and `join: { kind }` only, `GET …/code` answers 503 before the node is up, the record reaches `running` with `joinState` `awaiting`; the code's addresses are exactly `/dns4/localhost/tcp/<ws>/ws/p2p/<peerId>` while the node reports the TCP and loopback addresses too, and `decodeNodeClaimPayload` accepts it; a wrong secret is refused `claim-proof-invalid`; the right secret folds `claimed` with the requester's party and owner key on the record and in the list, the requester dials in (outbound on its side, a WebSocket connection on the node's), is in the node's authorized set, the node's signed record reaches the requester, `GET …/code` answers 409 `ALREADY_CLAIMED`, and no response but the code carried the secret; a rival owner is refused `already-claimed`; the child restarted on its volume reads `claimed` from `claim.json` ("Already claimed; CADRE_CLAIM_SECRET is ignored"), the record never regressed, the requester reconnects on a new connection, and neither run's output contains the secret; `DELETE` stops the record and the child.

**`provider-container-joins-by-invitation.integration.ts`.** The member is `startOwnerCliNode` with a `/ws` listener beside its TCP one; the provider has no public host anywhere. Steps: the owner mints an untargeted single-use invitation (`grantsOwner: false`, 15-minute expiry) naming its own addresses; `POST /containers { join: { kind: 'invitation', invitation } }` answers the bundle's party, the record reaches `running` and the fold reaches `{ kind: 'invitation', state: 'accepted', memberPeerId: <owner> }`; the owner lists the container as authorized and holds its signed record; a second container on the spent invitation reaches `running` with `joinState` `{ state: 'failed', retryable: false, error: …invite-spent… }` and the owner holds no row for it; no response and neither child's log carries the bundle; `DELETE` stops both.

**Harness.** `ProviderProcessOrchestrator.restartContainer(dockerId)`: stop the child and spawn it again with the same environment, ports and dockerId (`docker restart` in miniature), with the spawn extracted into one `spawnNode` shared with `createContainer`. `OwnerCliNode` gained `createInvitation` (`POST /admin/invites`), `isAuthorizedMember`, `listMembers`, and a `listenAddrs` option (default unchanged: one loopback TCP port).

**Docs.** `docs/testing.md`: both scenarios in the cross-process index line and one bullet each. `docs/architecture.md` → "Enrollment Flow: Phone Adds Provider Drone": a last bullet under the two join paths names the scenarios; the sentence that said the seed flow is "kept until the two are proven on the wire" now says `provider-retires-the-seed-path` removes it. The `provider-drone-phone-requester` sentence under the seed path stays.

## Where this departs from the ticket, and why

- **The scenarios drive the provider's HTTP routes, not `ContainerService` directly.** The ticket's steps "the response never carried the secret anywhere but the code" and "no response of `getContainerStatus` or `listContainers` carries the bundle" are false at the service layer by design: the record carries `join.secret` / `join.invitation`, and `redactContainer` in `routes.ts` is what strips them. Going through `createProviderServer` + `inject` proves the real routes, validator and redaction with the same `ContainerService` and enrollment loop underneath (`requireContainerRunning` is handed `server.containerService`). No port is bound.
- **Step 7 restarts with the same dockerId and ports (`restartContainer`), not `removeContainer` + a second `createContainer` under the same container id.** A re-create allocates new ports, so the announced `/ws` address and the requester's retained dial target would no longer match and the record's endpoints would go stale; Docker's own restart keeps both. `provider-seed-accepted` still re-creates and hand-patches the record for its own reason (dropping the pins).
- Step 5 of the claim scenario also waits for the node's signed record to reach the requester, which the ticket did not list; it is the "Control Network Sync" the architecture diagram ends on and took under a second here.

## Known gaps for the reviewer

- **"`not-ready` until the node reports an address" is only partly observed.** The 503 is pinned deterministically right after the create (no health endpoint yet). The window between the node reporting healthy with a peer id and announcing its `/ws` address was not seen on the run here: the first `GET …/code` after `running` answered the code. The scenario records every refusal seen before the code and asserts all were `503 NOT_AVAILABLE`, so an intervening refusal of another kind would fail it; the address-less window itself is not pinned.
- **`OwnerCliMemberRow.sig` reads a field the admin route does not document.** `GET /admin/members` is documented as `{ peerId, multiaddr }` but answers the control database's whole `CadrePeer` rows because `CadreNode.listMembers` returns `queryCadrePeers()` unchanged. The "container's own signed record reached the owner" check is `sig !== null` (the redemption writes the row with `Sig` null; the device's own publish signs it, confirmed in `control-database.ts` and `publishSelfRecord`). A `NOTE` on the field in `owner-cli-node.ts` says what to do if the route narrows.
- The invitation scenario's owner node is both the minter and the always-on member. In the household this flow serves they are two machines; nothing in the scenario depends on their being one, and the header says so.
- Timing budgets are the siblings' (`STARTUP_MS` 90 s, `OP_MS` 30 s); both files ran well inside them. Not run: the full integration suite.
- `@serfab/cadre-provider`'s `dist` was 27 seconds older than its `src` at the start of this ticket (the review commit of the prereq edited `container-service.ts` after its build) and was rebuilt with `yarn workspace @serfab/cadre-provider build`; nothing in it changed here.
