- `packages/integration-tests/test/formation-approval-real-fetch.spec.ts > createHttpFormationApprover against a real node:http server > times out a real socket that answers headers and then never sends a body` — was intermittent (~1 whole-file run in 10 on win32/Node 24), surfacing as a 60 s vitest timeout: Node's `fetch` (undici) occasionally drops the abort that lands while the response body is being read, and the pending read then neither rejects nor resolves until undici's own 300 s body timeout, so a hook that answers headers and goes quiet could hold a formation responder to that 300 s instead of the client's `timeoutMs`.
