## Resolved in place (no ticket — root cause landed)

- `packages/integration-tests/test/formation-approval-real-fetch.spec.ts > createHttpFormationApprover against a real node:http server > times out a real socket that answers headers and then never sends a body` — was intermittent (~1 whole-file run in 10 on win32/Node 24), surfacing as a 60 s vitest timeout: Node's `fetch` (undici) occasionally drops the abort that lands while the response body is being read, and the pending read then neither rejects nor resolves until undici's own 300 s body timeout, so a hook that answers headers and goes quiet could hold a formation responder to that 300 s instead of the client's `timeoutMs`. Fixed by `formation-approval-timeout-not-enforced` / this ticket in `packages/cadre-core/src/formation-approval.ts`: one per-request budget (`startBudget`/`ApprovalBudget`) now races every await that can outlive it — the `fetch` itself, each stream `read()`, and the readerless-path `response.text()` — against a deadline that fires independently of whether the runtime's abort is honoured; aborting is still attempted (it releases the socket where it works) but is no longer the sole bound. Verified 2026-07-31: `formation-approval-real-fetch.spec.ts` **10 whole-file runs, 10/10 green**, steady at 250-300 ms (was ~1 failure in 10 as a 60 s timeout before); `formation-approval.spec.ts` gained a deterministic repro (`stallingFetch()`: a 200 whose body stream never enqueues, never closes, and ignores every abort) covering both the timeout and caller-abort bounds without relying on the race.
