# A claimed node rejoins its new party without waiting

## Traced (this planning pass; counted from code, not measured)

The QR claim path:

1. B waits unclaimed. It builds a control database under a placeholder party and polls it, and throws it away when it restarts.
2. A delivers the seed (`/sereus/seed/1.0.0`): dial budget 21.5 s per address, 86 s per peer, then 28.5 s for the exchange.
3. A vouches B in its own store and starts a reconcile pass, which cannot reach B because B is restarting.
4. The cadre-cli restart waits on `seed:applied`, which waits on preferred-peer dials of up to 86 s each.
5. B restarts into A's party with a second cold schema apply. Its new store keeps no address for A (the seed's peers are not carried into the new party's bootstrap store; `start-node.ts` ~145 NOTE).
6. A reconnects only on its next 15 s pass, then pushes its store to B. B publishes its own record on its next 15 s pass.

## Design

1. **Carry the seed's peers into the new party's bootstrap store**, so the restarted B dials A at once.
2. **Do not wait on preferred-peer dials before restarting.** The restart needs the seed applied, not a connection; the restarted node dials on its own.
3. **B publishes its record on its first control connection** after the restart (with `new-machine-self-record-written-once`, possibly not at all if A already holds a current signed record from the seed exchange).
4. **A redials B on B's reconnection**, which `control-reconcile-woken-by-changes-reads-once` gives through connection events, not on the 15 s timer.
5. **The unclaimed placeholder database.** If B does not need a control database while unclaimed (read what the placeholder serves), do not build one.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **"Written while alone" counting.** The trace suggests the seed connection to the placeholder node is counted as a control connection (`cadre-node.ts` ~4521/~7970), which could run A's first-connection drain early and drop B's row from the re-send queue. That is inferred from code, not observed: confirm or rule it out first (it is `backlog/bug-seed-connection-counted-as-a-control-connection`); if confirmed, fix it here since the same lines change.
- **A claim whose seed reply is lost** (B applied, A did not see the ack): A's vouch stands; B's restart dials A from the carried peers.
- **A phone that is not reachable** when B restarts (relay-only phone, app backgrounded): B's dials fail and back off as today; nothing waits on them.
