# Invitations redeemable by any member: the old path is gone, every caller moved

Implemented in `ticket(implement): cadre-invitations-redeemable-by-any-member`; reviewed in `ticket(review): cadre-invitations-redeemable-by-any-member`.

## What landed

**cadre-core.** `SeedBootstrapService.createInvite`, `acceptPhone`, `encodeInvite`, `decodeInvite`, `dialInvite` and their `CadreNode` wrappers are deleted, with the types `CadreInvite`, `AddPhoneOptions`, `InviteResult`, the service's `inviteAddressResolver` config field, `CadreNode.openEnrollmentWindow`, the `enrollmentWindowUntil` field, `DEFAULT_ENROLLMENT_WINDOW_MS` and the window check in `admitInboundControlConnection` (the live-invitation check replaces it; the gate's checks are renumbered, and the blocked ticket `decide-cadre-invite-redeemed-before-the-row-replicates` names check 6). `CadreNodeConfig.network.inviteAddressResolver` and `setInviteAddresses` stay, read by `resolveInviteAddresses` for the invitation's own address. `addDrone`, `addPhoneWithRelay`, `authorizePeer`, `deliverSeed` and `applySeed` stay as the owner-online paths.

**cadre-cli.** The admin channel loses `POST /admin/accept-phone`; `POST /admin/invites` takes `{ peerId?, grantsOwner?, expiresInMs?, uses? }` (shape-checked; a bad field is `400 bad_request` naming it) and returns `{ invitation, encoded }`; `GET /admin/invites` lists every invitation the node holds with its standing; `DELETE /admin/invites/:key` withdraws. `cadre enroll invite [--peer-id <id>] [--owner] [--ttl <duration>] [--uses <n>]` prints the encoded bundle on stdout and a report on stderr, warning when `--owner` has no `--peer-id`. `cadre start --invitation <encoded>` refuses `--seed` and `--owner`, decodes and party-checks before anything starts, redeems right after `node.start()`, and keeps running on a failure as a failed `--seed` does. The admin-connection option parsing moved from `enroll add` into `admin-client.ts` so both enroll commands share it.

**cadre-host.** The trust circle is deleted outright: `src/auth/`, `routes/trust-circle.ts`, the `/auth/*` rows, `OwnerNodeClient.createInvite`/`acceptPhone`/`encodeInvite`, the `invite` and `trust` CLI commands and `--no-invite`, the installer's first-invite step, the Trust Circle UI page, `InviteModal.svelte`, the `trust-circle-changed` event, `trustCircle` on `/api/status`, and the `founder.trustCircle` wiring (`FounderServices` is `{ nat, strands }`). `duration.ts` moved to `src/donation/` for `grant issue --ttl`. Until `cadre-host-join-a-cadre` lands, an operator adds a device to a host-founded cadre with `cadre enroll invite` against the owner node's admin port (`docs/cadre-host.md` → "Adding a device to a host-founded cadre").

**Reference apps.** The "Paste enrollment invite (for trust)" input, `ownerKeysFromInvite` and `applySeed`'s pin argument are gone in both apps. Each gains a "Join a Cadre" section (`input-cadre-invitation`, `btn-join-cadre`) that runs `decodeCadreInvitation` then `CadreNode.redeemCadreInvitation`, shows "Joined cadre" naming the admitting member and whether the device became an owner, or "Join failed" with the refusal in plain words (`src/join-failure.ts` in each app, hand-copied twins by design). The seed field stays with a hint that a seed only works when the phone already trusts its signer. The RN device fixture and both `run-e2e.mjs` scripts pass `CADRE_INVITATION`; `_setup.yaml` pastes it and taps Join.

**Integration tests.** `control-bring-up-quiet-period`, `control-stream-authz` and `membership-connection-gater` open the stranger gate by minting a cadre invitation. `enrollment-e2e` replaces its three invite cases with: a device redeems at the owner itself and syncs; an expired invitation is refused with `invite-spent`. `cadre-host-trust-circle` is deleted; the harness drops the trust-circle fakes; `cadre-host-owner-node`'s add-then-remove cycle admits through `addDrone`.

**The scenario that proves the feature**: `cadre-invite-any-member.integration.ts`. Owner A (listening on loopback; storage kept across a restart) founds the cadre and admits always-on members M and N through `addDrone` plus `deliverSeed`; mints an untargeted owner-granting invitation; waits until both members hold the live row; stops; the scenario waits until A has left both members' FRET cohorts. Phone-shaped device P (no pinned key) redeems at a member: the result names the member and `grantsOwner`; P pinned A's key; the admitting member lists P as authorized and holds P's `OwnerKey` row; the other member lists P by replication; P lists the admitting member. A restarts over its kept stores, reconnects and lists P. A withdraws; once both members hold the tombstone, device Q is refused with `invite-spent`.

The scenario's shape deviates from the plan (an addressable owner, two always-on members) because of two defects found while writing it, each filed to `fix/`: `phone-owner-never-leaves-members-cohort` (an address-less owner is never evicted from the members' cohorts, so every member write waits on it for the whole commit budget) and `redemption-write-tears-on-a-member-whose-cohort-just-shrank` (a lone member's redemption write committed the usage row but not the membership rows once). Until they land, the household shape the feature is for (phone owner plus one always-on node) does not work, and the committed scenario does not exercise it.

**Docs.** `docs/architecture.md` (the invitation section names the headless and app surfaces; "Server Adds Phone" is a pointer; the seed-needed and "Which Side Dials" tables; the anchor, invitation and gate bullets; the helper block; the implementation-status lines), `docs/cadre-host.md`, `docs/reference-app-rn.md`, `docs/reference-app-ns.md`, `packages/cadre-cli/README.md` ("Join by invitation"), `packages/cadre-host/README.md`, root `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`.

No test was cut and none added: the defect found (the guard ordering) is two lines in an option-parsing block the handoff already declined to test, and was verified by running the command.
