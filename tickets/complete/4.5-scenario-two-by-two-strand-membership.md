# Membership actions from a party's non-founding machine — complete

New scenario `strand-membership-second-machine.integration.ts` (353 lines, one narrative
test): a closed strand across two parties × two machines (`bootTopology` 2×2
`genesis-first`, full control mesh, `joinStrandOn` with `type:'c'`, `founder:true`, all
four machines), with every membership writer call issued from machine index 1 of party B —
a machine that neither founded the strand nor is its party's owner.

Four phases: the founder's invite consumed on b[1]; both of party B's machines registering
device records under ONE member identity; the founder promoting party B's member and that
new manager then issuing an invitation and admitting a third key from b[1]; and last, a
single rejection (an unadmitted key issuing an invitation), per the closed-strand e2e's
rejection floor. Machine a[1] authors nothing and is gated on the full member set, the
rotated manager row, and both device records.

The scenario is deliberately visibility-only. Raw block-store coverage for this exact
topology already belongs to `strand-two-party-two-machine.integration.ts`; duplicating it
here would double the runtime to restate that file's claims.
