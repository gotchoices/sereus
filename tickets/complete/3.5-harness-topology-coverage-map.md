# Topology coverage map in `docs/testing.md`

`docs/testing.md` ends with a `## Topology coverage map` section: one bullet per network shape,
each naming a scenario under `packages/integration-tests/src/scenarios/` that exercises it, or
naming the shape uncovered and pointing at the open ticket for it. Reference material, not a
status board — no pass/fail state, consistent with the policy stated at the top of that file.
A closing paragraph points at the `TIME BUDGET` doc comment in
`packages/integration-tests/src/harness/topology.ts` for sizing a new scenario's hook timeouts,
rather than copying those numbers into a second place.

## Review findings

**Checked**: every scenario path and ticket slug in the section against the tree and the board;
whether the shapes claimed match what those scenarios actually build; whether shapes the suite
does exercise were left off; `yarn lint`; `yarn typecheck`.

**Major**: none. No new tickets filed — every finding was a wrong or missing line in one
section of one document, fixed in place.

**Minor — fixed in this pass** (all in the map section):

- The "cross-process nodes" line was wrong on both halves. It named
  `deliver-seed-cross-network.integration.ts`, which spawns no child process (it imports
  `TestCadreNetwork` plus a standalone `createLibp2p` node over TCP, all in one process), and
  credited `child-node-fixtures.ts`, which that scenario does not import — the only other
  reference to that module in the package is the `harness/index.ts` re-export, and its real
  consumers are `cadre-host-node-donation`, `cadre-host-owner-node`, and
  `provider-seed-accepted`. Rewrote the line around those three, and gave
  `deliver-seed-cross-network` its own accurate line (two separate libp2p networks in one
  process, over TCP rather than the suite's usual WebSocket).
- Relayed topology read as entirely uncovered. `relay-only-control-addr.integration.ts` covers
  the control plane — a control node with no inbound reachability reserving a circuit-relay slot
  on a sibling and being dialed through it — and the `strand-network-nat-relay-reachability`
  ticket itself lists that scenario under `files:` as existing coverage. Added the covered line
  and narrowed the uncovered one to the strand plane (a strand node earning its own reservation,
  separately from its control node's), which is what that ticket is actually about.
- `multi-party-sync.integration.ts` was unlisted: two parties, each an owner plus a drone, and
  its own header states it deliberately stops short of cross-party strand transport. That is the
  nearest shape to the first uncovered class, so its absence made that class harder to judge,
  not easier. Added, with the boundary spelled out.
- The opening sentence claimed to say what the suite exercises while naming 15 of the 44
  scenario files. Added a sentence scoping it: one scenario per shape, not every scenario, and
  scenarios whose subject is a protocol or service rather than a shape are not listed.

**Verified correct as written, left alone**: all 15 originally-named scenario files exist; all
four uncovered ticket slugs resolve (`scenario-two-multi-machine-cadres-share-one-strand` in
`tickets/plan/`, the other three in `tickets/backlog/`); the `TIME BUDGET` note exists at the
`topology.ts` module header and says what the doc attributes to it;
`strand-formation-e2e.integration.ts` is correctly placed as in-process (its only `cadre-cli`
mentions are in a comment explaining why it does *not* depend on that package).

**Tripwire — recorded, not ticketed**: the map is hand-maintained and nothing checks its paths
or slugs, so a renamed scenario or a landed ticket leaves a stale line. Parked as a `NOTE:` at
the end of the section itself, with the condition for acting on it — generate the covered half
from the scenario directory if the map starts being read as authoritative or the stale lines
outnumber the live ones.

**Considered and declined**: none — no accepted-tradeoff `NOTE:` exists at this site.
