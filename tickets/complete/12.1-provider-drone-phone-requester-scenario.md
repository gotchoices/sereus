# Complete: a phone-shaped requester rents a provider node

## What landed

`packages/integration-tests/src/scenarios/provider-drone-phone-requester.integration.ts` runs the "Phone Adds Provider Drone" enrollment flow end to end. The provider side is an in-process `ContainerService` + `MemoryStore` + `ProviderProcessOrchestrator` spawning a real `cadre-cli` child from the provider's own `buildNodeEnv`. The requester is an in-process phone-shaped `CadreNode` (`listenAddrs: []`, WebSocket + circuit-relay only). Its six ordered steps:

1. The requester is up and listens on nothing.
2. A container created with `bootstrapNodes: []` and the requester's owner key pinned reaches `running`.
3. The peer info carries the announced `/dns4/localhost` TCP and `/ws` pair.
4. `addDrone` mints the seed and the provider delivers it.
5. The requester dials in over `/ws` and is in the node's authorized member set.
6. `terminateContainer` stops the record and the child.

Harness changes:

- `ProviderProcessOrchestrator` gained `publicHost` (announce addresses, as `DockerOrchestrator`), a test-only `adminChannel`, and `logTail(containerId)`.
- `requireContainerRunning` was hoisted out of `provider-seed-accepted`.
- `buildPhoneOwner` was hoisted into `node-fixtures.ts`, replacing the copies in `cadre-host-join-by-qr` and `node-claim-by-phone`.
- `adminClient` is exported from `owner-cli-node.ts`.

`docs/testing.md` and `docs/architecture.md` name the scenario and what it does not yet check.

Deferred: the node's own signed address record reaching the requester. A seeded node publishes it only if its row replicated in before a 1-second one-shot, and otherwise on the 7.5-minute heartbeat. `tickets/fix/seeded-node-publishes-its-address-record-late.md` (repro: verified) owns the fix and the assertion, and a `NOTE:` at step 5 points to it.

## Review findings

Read the `ticket(implement): provider-drone-phone-requester-scenario` diff first, then the handoff.

**Fixed inline (minor):**

- DRY: `logTail` added a third "last N lines of a child's node.log, never throw" reader. The others were `readLogTail` in `owner-cli-node.ts` and the local `logTail` in `node-claim-by-phone`. I added one `readLogTail(path, lines)` to `child-node-fixtures.ts`, the shared module for child-process fixtures, and made both harness readers use it. `owner-cli-node`'s version also gained the never-throw guarantee: before, it checked `existsSync` and could still throw on a read error. `getLogs` stays separate on purpose, since it mirrors `docker logs` and throws for a forgotten handle. `node-claim-by-phone`'s local `logTail` sits on its `readLog`, which the scenario also uses for content assertions, so I left it alone.

**Checked, no change needed:**

- Correctness of the scenario steps:
  - Step 1's `registerSelf() !== 'skipped'` matches the `'inserted' | 'refreshed' | 'skipped'` outcome type.
  - Step 4's `applySeed` retry has no side effects to repeat. `ContainerService.applySeed` is a read plus a POST, and a refusal is reported in the failure message through the last result.
  - Step 5's `isAuthorizedOnNode` throwing inside `waitUntil` is retried, because `waitUntil` logs a condition that throws and keeps polling.
- The `/dns4/localhost` public host is the right choice. With `127.0.0.1`, step 3 would pass even if the announce variable never reached the child.
- The fix ticket filed by implement has a root-cause site (`publishSelfRecordOnceClaimed`), an anchor, two fix options with their costs, and a "done when" that rules out passing by timing. It is in `fix/`, as a verified bug should be.
- The hoists (`buildPhoneOwner`, `requireContainerRunning`, `adminClient`) are exact supersets of the copies they replaced. No caller changed behaviour.
- Docs: the `testing.md` cross-process index and the new bullet match the code. They also fix the old index's pointer to a bullet that did not exist. The `architecture.md` key point states the gap and names the ticket.
- Resource cleanup: `afterAll` stops the requester and any leftover child and removes the temp root. The temp root holds only provider volumes and no `node_modules`, so the recursive delete cannot follow a junction. The bare `catch { /* ignore */ }` in teardown is the suite-wide convention (24 sites), so I did not re-litigate it here.

**Known gaps accepted (from the handoff, weighed and kept):**

- Step 5 proves "dialable over `/ws` from the peer info", not "dialable at the announced address specifically". Pinning that would need address filtering, which the ticket excluded.
- `/dns4/localhost` depends on the dialer resolving it to IPv4 loopback. The bound `/ip4` addresses are tried too, so a `::1` answer would not fail the step.
- The admin channel is a test-only env layer that the provider never produces. It is documented as layer 5 in the orchestrator header.

**Tests:** no tests added or cut. The new scenario is the ticket's deliverable, and each of its steps pins a part of the specified flow. The harness changes are glue that the scenarios exercise.

**Tickets filed by review:** none. **Tripwires added:** none. The only conditional concern, `/dns4/localhost` resolution, is already stated in the scenario header and in these findings.
