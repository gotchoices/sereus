# Complete: the cohort read deadline derived from the link, first sync re-measured

## What changed

**The derivation.** `cohortReadDeadlineMs(linkRoundTripMs?)` in `link-budget.ts` is `CIRCUIT_REQUEST_ROUND_TRIPS` (2) times the resolved link round trip: 7 000 ms at the default 3 500. No admission allowance, because a read runs over an open connection. Optimystic's whole-reconcile-pass bound becomes `max(5000, 5 × 7000)` = 35 000 ms.

**One helper for both policy sites.** `declaredCohortReadDeadlineMs(network)` returns the host's explicit `cohortQueryTimeoutMs`, else the derivation at the host's `linkRoundTripMs`, else `undefined`. Both `CadreNode.buildControlNodeOptions` and `StrandInstanceManager.buildStrandRuntime` pass its result to the plugin's policy builders. `undefined` keeps the builders returning the frozen base policy by identity, which the existing identity specs pin, and is correct because the plugin constant equals the derivation at the default declaration. Both sites already called `resolveLinkRoundTripMs` eagerly before building the policy, so an invalid declaration still fails at start, not inside the policy build.

**The plugin constant.** `COHORT_READ_DEADLINE_MS` is 7 000. Its doc states the derivation it must equal, keeps the 5 000 history (why it was chosen, the 18-versus-2 declined reads at the 1.8 s band), and records the new measurement. The plugin cannot import cadre-core, so the number is spelled out.

**The guard.** `link-budget.spec.ts` pins `COHORT_READ_DEADLINE_MS === cohortReadDeadlineMs()`. That is the one fact two packages must agree on.

**The directive vocabulary.** A fourth `LINK_DEADLINE_GUARD` reason, `measured, not derived: <where the measurement lives>`, is documented in the four places the third was (`eslint.config.mjs` comment, `link-budget.ts` module doc, architecture.md's lint paragraph, testing.md's "Lint coverage"), and `LINK_DEADLINE_MESSAGE` names it. `DEFAULT_STRAND_FIRST_SYNC_TIMEOUT_MS` carries it now instead of `link-bound, not yet derived: <this ticket>`. No directive or prose outside `tickets/complete/` and the run logs names this ticket's slug any more.

**Prose.** Every place that quoted 5 000 ms or 25 s as the read deadline or the pass bound now states the derivation, 7 000 ms, or 35 s: `types.ts`, `control-read-retry.ts` ("more than four times this budget"), `control-write-retry.ts`, `compose-strand.ts`, `cadre-cli`'s config types, the eslint scope sentence, architecture.md (the shared-policy paragraph, the lint paragraph, the known-limits bullets, the config snippet), cadre-consistency.md (the ladder intro and the checked-relationships bullet, now "checked by `link-budget.spec.ts`"), testing.md, and reference-app-rn.md.

## The finding that changed the ticket's premise

The ticket's `repro: static` said that at the supported link every consult times out at 5 s. What the logs show is different: in both comparison runs every consult on the joiner ended 3.00 to 3.02 s after the one before it, at 5 000 and 7 000 ms alike, and all of them after the strand nodes had been connected for 15 s or more. A consult that completed would need at least two link round trips, 6 s, so none ran to an answer, and neither cohort deadline ever fired.

The 3 000 ms is Optimystic's `DEFAULT_DIAL_TIMEOUT_MS`. `sync/client.ts` (`SyncClient.requestBlock`, called by `clusterLatestCallback` in `libp2p-node-base.ts`) applies it to the latest-revision query through `withRpcDeadlineDefaults`, `protocol-client.ts` turns it into the dial abort signal, and `open-protocol-stream.ts` forwards that signal into `newStream` on the connection-reuse path, where the full multistream-select negotiation costs one link round trip, 3 s here. No node option reaches it. So at the supported link the derived deadline is never reached and the 39-versus-32 difference is noise; the join completes because peer-join backfill delivers the blocks and the first-sync gate probes until it holds them. The derivation is still right (it is what a consult needs once the upstream deadline lets its negotiation through, and the 1.8 s-band data shows the per-peer deadline doing its work where negotiation fits inside 3 s), but its effect is not observable at 1 500 ms one-way until upstream moves.

Recorded on `COHORT_READ_DEADLINE_MS`, in `link-budget.ts`'s "What still fails" (whose sentence "a request over an open connection does not dial" was wrong for the negotiation part), in architecture.md's known-limits bullet, and as `tickets/blocked/report-request-dial-deadline-cuts-cohort-consults-on-open-connections-to-optimystic`, which carries proposed text for the upstream ticket's open question. Sibling repositories are read-only, so a human has to paste it.
