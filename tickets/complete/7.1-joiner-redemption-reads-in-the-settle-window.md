# Record that the settle window's redemption cost is one redemption

## What changed

One paragraph added to the "── Baseline ──" doc comment of `message-cost-accounting.integration.ts`, after the strand-row paragraph: 38 of the strand settle window's 73 gets (33 `consumeInvite`, 5 `registerMemberPeer`) and B's 20 `/cluster` streams to A are B's single invitation redemption plus its `MemberPeer` binding commit. They land in the settle window because "A sees B insert" waits for B's `App.Seat` row, not its `Strand.Member` row, and the membership reconciler redeems in the background. `DEBUG='sereus:cadre:strand-membership*'` shows one `Consumed invite` and no retry.

## Testing

Comment-only change; no build, test or re-measurement. The numbers come from the traced run recorded in the fix stage (2026-10-20, `MESSAGE_COST_CASE=strand`).

## Not done (by design)

- No phase was added to wait for membership: it would move counts between phases and make the baseline incomparable.
- The 33 reads inside one `consumeInvite` (repeated `Invite` row lookups by the strand schema's checks) were not ticketed: a one-time cost per join. A transaction-scoped block cache in Optimystic would be the general fix if join cost ever matters.
