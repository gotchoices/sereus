# Derive the per-peer cohort read deadline from the declared link, and re-measure first sync

## The defect

`COHORT_READ_DEADLINE_MS` (5 000 ms, `packages/quereus-plugin-sereus/src/cluster-size.ts`) is how long one cohort peer gets to answer one read-path request. It feeds both networks' `clusterPolicy.cohortQueryTimeoutMs`, and Optimystic derives its whole-reconcile-pass bound from it (`reconcilePassTimeoutMs = max(5000, 5 × per-peer)`, `../optimystic/packages/db-p2p/src/cluster/cluster-policy.ts`). It is a deadline over exchanges on the machine-to-machine link, but it is written as milliseconds rather than derived from `NetworkConfig.linkRoundTripMs`.

One read-path request opens a fresh protocol stream over an already-open connection (`@optimystic/db-p2p`'s `openProtocolStream`). It costs a protocol negotiation plus the request and its answer, which is `CIRCUIT_REQUEST_ROUND_TRIPS` (2) link round trips (`link-budget.ts`). At the supported 3 s round trip that is 6 s of pure delay, above 5 s. So at the supported link, every consult times out and the read has nothing to corroborate against. That is the optimystic #22 failure, which the 5 000 ms value was chosen to end at the old 1.8 s band.

`repro: static`: arithmetic from the counts in `link-budget.ts`, not a measurement at that link. The re-measure below observes it: count `cluster-fetch:peers-silent` lines at 1 500 ms one-way before and after the change.

A request that must first dial is bounded by Optimystic's own fixed dial deadline, not by this one (`debt-rpc-dial-deadlines-cannot-open-a-slow-relayed-connection`, upstream). That is out of scope here.

## The change

- **`cohortReadDeadlineMs(linkRoundTripMs?) = CIRCUIT_REQUEST_ROUND_TRIPS × resolveLinkRoundTripMs(linkRoundTripMs)`** in `link-budget.ts`. That is 7 000 ms at the default 3 500. No admission allowance: a read runs over an open connection and opens none.
- **Plugin constant becomes 7 000**, with a doc stating it equals `CIRCUIT_REQUEST_ROUND_TRIPS × DECLARED_LINK_ROUND_TRIP_MS` in cadre-core. The plugin cannot import cadre-core, because the dependency runs the other way. Keep the measurement history in its doc (5 000 was measured at the 1.8 s band; 18 declined reads at 1 000 ms against 2 at 5 000), and add the new derivation and whatever the re-measure shows. Optimystic's whole-pass bound becomes 35 s. Say so where the doc says 25 s.
- **cadre-core passes the derived value only when the host declared a link**: `cohortQueryTimeoutMs: network?.cohortQueryTimeoutMs ?? (network?.linkRoundTripMs === undefined ? undefined : cohortReadDeadlineMs(network.linkRoundTripMs))`, at both sites (`cadre-node.ts` control policy, `strand-instance-manager.ts` strand policy). With nothing declared, the frozen base policy object is still returned by identity. `cadre-node-control-node-options.spec.ts` (~142, 199, 208, 260) and `plugin.spec.ts` (~720) pin that identity, so this form keeps them green. It is also exactly right: undeclared means the default declaration, and the plugin constant equals the derivation at the default. Put this in a small helper beside the other link-budget helpers, so the two sites cannot drift. An explicit `cohortQueryTimeoutMs` still wins.
- **One new assertion** in `link-budget.spec.ts`: `COHORT_READ_DEADLINE_MS === cohortReadDeadlineMs()`. This is the one fact two packages must agree on, and nothing else catches a drift. It is the load-bearing guard the original ticket asked for.
- **The directive vocabulary.** `DEFAULT_STRAND_FIRST_SYNC_TIMEOUT_MS` cannot be derived from a round-trip count: it is a whole sync over many consults. Add a fourth `LINK_DEADLINE_GUARD` reason, `measured, not derived: <where the measurement lives>`, in the same four places the prereq documented the third (`eslint.config.mjs` comment, `link-budget.ts` module doc, architecture.md lint paragraph, `docs/testing.md` → "Lint coverage"), and extend the `LINK_DEADLINE_MESSAGE` sentence that names the other reasons. Replace its `link-bound, not yet derived: cohort-read-deadline-derived-from-the-link` directive with that reason. After this ticket, no directive or prose in the repo names this ticket's slug.
- **eslint.config.mjs's scope sentence** ("`COHORT_READ_DEADLINE_MS` in quereus-plugin-sereus is owned by …") becomes: the plugin constant is pinned equal to cadre-core's derivation by `link-budget.spec.ts`. The scope stays cadre-core only.

## Re-measuring first sync

`DEFAULT_STRAND_FIRST_SYNC_TIMEOUT_MS` (300 s) was sized from runs at 900 ms one-way (a 1.8 s round trip) and a 5 000 ms read deadline. Its doc records the bands and says to re-measure before either number moves. A consult against a peer that cannot answer now costs 7 s instead of 5 s, and the supported link is now 3 s. Measure at the new value:

```
REATTACH_SYNC_MEASURE=1 REATTACH_DELAY_MS=1500 REATTACH_ARMS=fresh REATTACH_RUNS=2 DEBUG=sereus:cadre:strand-first-sync yarn workspace @serfab/integration-tests exec vitest run strand-reattach-first-sync-measure 2>&1 | tee tickets/.logs/cohort-read-deadline-derived-from-the-link.fresh.log
```

Then the same with `REATTACH_ARMS=reattach-kept REATTACH_RUNS=3`. Run in the foreground, one arm per command. The `DEBUG` output streams and keeps the runner's idle timer alive. The scenario's per-run cap is `WRITABLE_WAIT_MS` (600 s). Stale-build guard: this runs against sibling `dist` builds. If the guard reports a sibling as stale, stop and record the ticket as blocked on that sibling's build (tickets/rules/sibling-repos.md). Never build it.

- **If the runs complete at 1 500 ms**, those are the new bands. Also count `cluster-fetch:peers-silent` in one run at `REATTACH_COHORT_READ_MS=5000` against one at the new default. That is the observation behind `repro: static` above. Add `optimystic:db-p2p:coordinator-repo*` to `DEBUG` for those two runs only.
- **If runs at 1 500 ms break before sync** (the earlier 900 ms note says runs above it broke on redialling; the dial budgets have since been derived, so this may no longer hold), record where they break, fall back to 900 ms one-way at the new 7 000 ms deadline, and record both facts.
- **Decision rule for the 300 s budget**: keep 300 s if the worst harness sample is at most 100 s, which keeps the ≥3x margin the doc argues for. Otherwise raise it to 3 × the worst sample, rounded up to the next whole minute, and state the new cost the way the existing accepted-tradeoff NOTE does. The two external samples (reporter's ~150 s re-attach, the Galaxy S7's 178 s fresh join) stay in the doc as they are. Neither can be re-run here.
- Record the runs in the constant's doc in its existing form: date, machine, delay and mode, deadline, per-arm figures. Replace the "NOTE: this number and the first-sync budget are coupled" paragraph in `cluster-size.ts` only if it no longer reads true.

If the measurement cannot be completed (sibling build stale, the scenario broken at both delays), still land the derivation, and keep the first-sync directive as `link-bound, not yet derived:` pointing at a new backlog ticket `debt-first-sync-budget-unmeasured-at-supported-link` that you file with what you saw. Don't leave a directive naming this ticket's slug.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **Frozen-policy identity** when nothing is declared: covered by the existing specs listed above. Run them.
- **Explicit `cohortQueryTimeoutMs` plus a declared link**: the explicit value wins. That is the precedence the `??` gives. Say so in the `types.ts` doc.
- **Invalid `linkRoundTripMs`**: `resolveLinkRoundTripMs` throws. Both sites already call it eagerly at start, so no new failure path appears. Confirm the eager call precedes the policy build at both sites.
- **Control and strand networks must get the same value**, because they ride one link. Both sites use the one helper.
- **compose-strand's plugin-only path** (`compose-strand.ts` ~249) takes the frozen policy whole. It now gets 7 000. Update its NOTE, which says "5000 ms is the right answer".
- **Other prose quoting 5 000 / 5 s / 25 s** as the read deadline or the whole-pass bound: `control-read-retry.ts` (~70-80), `types.ts`, `strand-first-sync-gate.ts`, `link-budget.ts` (the "What still fails" bullet and `DECLARED_LINK_ROUND_TRIP_MS`'s "Two budgets outside this module … do NOT move with it", which stops being true for the first), architecture.md's known-limits bullets, and the prereq's doc section (its checked-relationships line for the plugin/cadre-core equality). grep `COHORT_READ_DEADLINE_MS` and `5000 ms`/`5 000 ms`/`25 s` across `packages/*/src` and `docs`.
- **The admission gate and read-retry budget get further below the read deadline** (2 s and 1.5 s against 7 s). That is expected: both cut off by design (prereq). Their comments quote "5000 ms" and "more than three times this budget"; update the figures.
- **`strand-instance-manager-cluster-size.spec.ts` ~156** asserts `STRAND_CLUSTER_POLICY.cohortQueryTimeoutMs` equals the constant. It stays green. Leave it.
