# Retire the provider's seed path

Last ticket of the `provider-client-and-sign-up` chain. The decision is recorded in `provider-container-joins-by-claim-or-invitation`: the claim and invitation paths replace `pinnedOwnerKeys` + `GET /peer` + `addDrone` + `PUT /seed`, and the project keeps no backwards compatibility. `provider-join-scenarios-phone-shaped-requester` proved both replacements on the wire before this runs.

## What goes

- `POST /containers`: `join` becomes required; `partyId`, `bootstrapNodes` and `pinnedOwnerKeys` are gone from the body (the invitation supplies the party, a claim the placeholder; an invitation names the members to dial; nobody pins keys). `owner-key-validation.ts` and `bootstrap-node-validation.ts` and their tests go, and with them the `bootstrapNodesOptional` route flag: a claim requires `docker.publicHost` and an invitation does not, which the validator says in its own words.
- `PUT /containers/:id/seed`, `ContainerService.applySeed`, `Scope.ContainersSeed`, `Container.seedEndpoint`, `Container.seedToken`, `OrchestratorCreateResult.seedToken` and `seedEndpoint`, `CADRE_SEED_TOKEN` from `buildNodeEnv` (an absent variable means the node registers no seed route, per `container-env.ts`'s NOTE), `NodeEnvSpec.seedToken`, `redactContainer`'s seed-token clause (the redaction of `join` stays), `Container.pinnedOwnerKeys`, `OrchestratorCreateRequest.pinnedOwnerKeys` and `bootstrapNodes` (`bootstrapNodes` was the requester's address list for the container to dial; an invitation carries that list now).
- `GET /containers/:id/peer` stays: it is read-only and the app shows addresses from it.
- cadre-host's `HostProcessOrchestrator` implements the same `OrchestratorCreateRequest`; remove its handling of the two fields if it still has any (its hosted nodes join by claim or invitation already, so nothing calls it with them), and nothing else in cadre-host.
- Integration: delete `provider-seed-accepted.integration.ts` and `provider-drone-phone-requester.integration.ts` (their claims are covered by the two new scenarios); `ProviderProcessOrchestrator` drops the seed token and bootstrap list; `requireContainerRunning` and the other hoisted helpers stay where they are used.
- `tickets/backlog/debt-provider-seed-endpoint-token-pairing-untyped.md` describes the pairing of two fields that no longer exist: delete it and say so in the handoff.

## Docs

- `packages/cadre-provider/README.md`: remove "Seed trust: pin the tenant's owner key at create time" and "Bootstrap addresses are checked at create time"; rewrite "Phones dial containers at the public host" around the claim; the API table and scopes table lose the seed row.
- `docs/architecture.md`: "Enrollment Flow: Phone Adds Provider Drone" keeps only the claim and invitation sequences; "Provider Flow" likewise; "When Is a Seed Needed?" row 1 becomes the claim (a claim is a seed with a proof of the secret, delivered by the phone over libp2p; the provider carries none); row 4 of "Which Side Dials" names the two ways in; the sentence in Provider Integration's intro that says the provider never has owner keys now holds without qualification. `docs/testing.md` drops the two deleted scenarios from its index.
- The cadre-cli Docker files (`docker-compose.yml`, `env.example`) are not the provider's and keep `CADRE_SEED_TOKEN` and `CADRE_OWNER_KEYS` for operators who run the image by hand; leave them.

## Edge cases & interactions

- A `FileStore` written by an earlier provider holds `seedToken`, `seedEndpoint` and `pinnedOwnerKeys` on old records: the store's loader drops unknown fields or keeps them inert; verify which by reading `loadCache`, and if it keeps them, strip the three on load so a record never carries a field the type lacks. By inspection, one line if needed.
- A customer with an old seed-path container still running: it has no `join` and no `joinState`; `GET /containers/:id` shows it, `DELETE` works, `/code` answers `NOT_CLAIMABLE`. State this in the README's deployment section as the upgrade note, in one sentence.
- `enrollIfHealthy` and the reap sweep do not depend on the seed fields. By inspection.
- `provider-container-claimed-by-phone` and `provider-container-joins-by-invitation` must stay green after the harness change: run both.

## Tests

No new tests; delete those of the removed modules, and the redaction test keeps asserting that neither the claim secret nor the invitation bundle leaks.
