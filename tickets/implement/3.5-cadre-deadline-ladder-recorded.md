# Record the ladder between cadre's deadlines and Optimystic's, and settle each site

## Background

Optimystic (the replicated-block layer under cadre's databases) gives each cohort peer a per-peer deadline to answer one read-path request (`clusterPolicy.cohortQueryTimeoutMs`), and derives a whole-reconcile-pass bound from it: `max(5000, 5 × per-peer)`. Sereus declares the per-peer deadline as `COHORT_READ_DEADLINE_MS` = 5 000 ms in `packages/quereus-plugin-sereus/src/cluster-size.ts`, so the whole-pass bound is 25 000 ms. Before that declaration they were Optimystic's defaults, 1 000 and 5 000 ms.

Cadre-core has its own deadlines layered over reads and commits, chosen against the old pair. The plan pass examined every one. The findings below are settled; this ticket writes them down and applies them. It changes no numbers. The two numeric changes that came out of the audit are separate tickets:

- `dial-budgets-contain-the-admission-decision` (the admission decision is spent inside every dial into a gated node, and no dial budget counts it).
- `cohort-read-deadline-derived-from-the-link` (the per-peer read deadline itself is fixed milliseconds, below two link round trips at the supported link; re-measuring the first-sync wait belongs with it).

## Decisions, per site

Each cadre-core deadline that waits on an Optimystic read or commit means one of two things. It **cuts off** the inner work (a caller cannot wait longer, and the outcome on expiry is designed), or it **contains** the inner work (it must be longer than what it waits on). The audit's answers:

| deadline | value | waits on | intent | decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ADMISSION_DECISION_TIMEOUT_MS` (`membership-connection-gater.ts`) | 2 000 ms | the inbound admission policy: `listAuthorizedMembers`, two live control reads (`Revocation`, then `CadrePeer`) | cuts off | Keep 2 000. See "Why the admission deadline stays short" below. |
| `CONTROL_READ_RETRY_BUDGET_MS` (`control-read-retry.ts`) | 1 500 ms | the read retry loop, checked between attempts | cuts off | Keep. A `cohort-unreachable` read that burned a whole per-peer deadline is deliberately not retried: the caller that sets this budget (the admission gate) has already taken its fail-open answer by then. The retry still covers the fast failures it exists for: the ~25 ms transactor read-phase aggregate, and a `cohort-unreachable` during bring-up, which fails fast because there is no connection to ask. `control-read-retry.spec.ts` already pins `CONTROL_READ_RETRY_BUDGET_MS < ADMISSION_DECISION_TIMEOUT_MS`. |
| `CONTROL_WRITE_RETRY_BUDGET_MS` (`control-write-retry.ts`) | 10 000 ms | the write retry loop, checked between attempts | cuts off | Keep. What changed: an attempt whose read phase consulted a silent peer now ends at about one per-peer deadline (5 s) rather than 1 s, so it still gets one retry inside 10 s, where before it got two. An attempt that hits the 10 s `ClusterClient` response deadline on a silent cohort member still gets none. Both are the designed shape: the budget ends retries of slow attempts. |
| `DEFAULT_PROVISION_TIMEOUT_MS` (`strand-formation-protocol.ts`) | 12 000 ms | the formation provisioning hook: control reads, one `FormationUsage` commit, the approval hook | contains, with a designed cut-off | Not decided here. It is one layer of the formation deadline ordering (step < provision < initiator < session), which `debt-formation-and-relay-admission-deadlines-ignore-the-declared-link` already owns; the Optimystic part of it is appended to that ticket. Retarget its directive to that slug. |
| `DEFAULT_SEED_READ_TIMEOUT_MS` (`seed-bootstrap.ts`) | 10 000 ms | one inbound seed frame on a stream the sender already opened | neither | Reclassify. It wraps no Optimystic read or commit: `verifyAndMergeSeed` writes only libp2p's peer store, and it runs after the read, not inside it. It is the same receiver cap as `DEFAULT_WAKE_READ_TIMEOUT_MS` and `DEFAULT_ADDR_READ_TIMEOUT_MS`, which already carry `link-independent`. Use their wording. |
| `DEFAULT_CONTROL_COHORT_RECONCILE_MS` (`control-cohort.ts`) | 15 000 ms | nothing. It is a timer interval | not a deadline | No change to the number. `reconcileControlCohort` is single-flight, so a pass longer than the interval is joined, not stacked, and the effective cadence becomes the pass length. Say so on the constant. Also correct the comment in `runReconcileControlCohort` (~3079), which says the per-stream gate snapshot's staleness is bounded "to the reconcile interval". It is bounded by the time between pass starts, which is the longer of the interval and the previous pass. One unreachable sibling's dial alone is `dialBudget.totalMs` (56 s at the default). |
| `DEFAULT_STRAND_FIRST_SYNC_TIMEOUT_MS` (`strand-first-sync-gate.ts`) | 300 000 ms | a whole first sync, several consults | contains | Measured, and re-measured when the read deadline moved. It was sized at a 1.8 s round trip and at the 5 000 ms per-peer deadline, and both are about to move. Retarget its directive to `cohort-read-deadline-derived-from-the-link`, which re-measures it. |
| wake / strand-address attempt deadlines (`strand-wake-protocol.ts`, `strand-addr-protocol.ts`) | derived, 21 s | dial + request, plus the receiver's `isMember` (two live control reads) | contains, except for the membership read | Leave the membership read uncounted and turn both NOTEs into tripwires that point at the new doc section (text below). |

### Why the admission deadline stays short

Three reasons, all of which go into the doc section and the constant's comment:

- **Fail-open is the designed outcome.** A slow decision admits a connection only. The per-protocol stream gates still refuse every members-only protocol from the materialized snapshot, and unplaced relay reservations stay capped (`MAX_UNAUTHORIZED_RELAY_RESERVATIONS`).
- **The slow case is bring-up, not steady state.** A membership read consults the cohort only for a block it does not hold, or before the `Revocation` ledger marker exists. After the marker exists, every block those reads touch is held and none of them consults (`control-founding-consult-budget.spec.ts` pins both states). So the original ticket's "on a slow link the gate's answer is always the fail-open one" overstates it. The gate always fails open only on a node whose membership reads still consult.
- **The decision is spent on the dialing machine's clock.** libp2p's listener calls `denyInboundEncryptedConnection` before it answers the multiplexer negotiation, and the dialer is waiting on that negotiation (`node_modules/libp2p/dist/src/upgrader.js`: the gate at ~189, the dialer's `mss.select` for the muxer at ~301). Every millisecond the gate spends therefore adds to the other machine's dial. A relayed dial at the supported 3 s round trip measured 12 094 ms against a 14 000 ms budget, so there is about 1.9 s of room. Raising this deadline trades a narrower fail-open window for dials that time out. `dial-budgets-contain-the-admission-decision` makes the dial budgets carry the decision explicitly.

## The third directive reason

`LINK_DEADLINE_GUARD` (`eslint.config.mjs`) accepts two disable reasons today: `link-independent: <why>` and `link-bound, not yet derived: <slug>`. A cut-off deadline is neither. It does wait on link work, through an Optimystic read, but its value comes from what its caller can tolerate, so it must not grow with the link. Add a third reason and document it in the three places that list the reasons (the `eslint.config.mjs` comment, the `link-budget.ts` module doc, and the architecture.md lint paragraph):

`// eslint-disable-next-line no-restricted-syntax -- cuts off by design: <why>; see docs/cadre-consistency.md → "Deadlines Over Optimystic's Reads and Commits"`

Apply it to `ADMISSION_DECISION_TIMEOUT_MS`, `CONTROL_READ_RETRY_BUDGET_MS` and `CONTROL_WRITE_RETRY_BUDGET_MS`. The doc section's table must list exactly the sites that carry it. Grepping `cuts off by design` is how a reviewer checks that.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **Unused-directive gate.** `reportUnusedDisableDirectives: 'error'`: each directive whose reason text changes must still sit directly above a flagged literal. Verified by `yarn lint`.
- **`DEFAULT_CONTROL_COHORT_RECONCILE_MS` is not flagged** (its name ends `RECONCILE_MS`) and must not gain a directive. A directive with nothing to disable fails lint.
- **Seed read reclassification.** Confirm by inspection that nothing between `readSeedFrame` and the `readStreamToEnd` timeout touches the control database. `handleSeedStream` checks the concurrency cap, then reads. The trust-policy evaluation and peer-store merge come after the read and are outside this deadline.
- **Both gaters share the admission deadline.** `strand-revocation-enforcer.ts` imports `ADMISSION_DECISION_TIMEOUT_MS` for the closed-strand revoked-peer gate. Its decision reads the strand's revocation state, not the control database. The doc row should say the deadline covers both gaters.
- **No number changes.** Every spec pinning these values stays green unchanged. If one breaks, a number was edited by mistake.
- **After the edit, the old slug must not appear** anywhere under `packages/*/src`, `packages/*/test`, `docs`, `eslint.config.mjs`, or the open ticket folders. `dist/` copies are build output and are ignored.
