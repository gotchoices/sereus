# Dial budgets contain the listener's admission decision

## The defect

A control node gates every inbound connection in libp2p's `denyInboundEncryptedConnection` hook (`membership-connection-gater.ts`, and on closed strands `strand-revocation-enforcer.ts`). The decision is raced against `ADMISSION_DECISION_TIMEOUT_MS` (2 000 ms) and fails open on expiry. libp2p's upgrader runs that hook before the listener answers the stream-multiplexer negotiation, and the dialer is blocked on that negotiation (`node_modules/libp2p/dist/src/upgrader.js`: the gate at ~189, the dialer's `mss.select` for the muxer at ~301). So the gate's time is spent on the dialer's dial clock.

`link-budget.ts` budgets a relayed dial at `RELAYED_DIAL_ROUND_TRIPS` (4) × the declared round trip, which is 14 000 ms at the default 3 500. The measured relayed dial at the supported 3 s round trip is 12 094 ms. That leaves about 1.9 s of room, and the gate is allowed 2 s. A slow decision (a membership read that consults the cohort, which happens during bring-up; see the prereq's doc section) therefore pushes a good dial past its budget, and the dial fails as if the peer were absent. The same applies to the listener's `inboundUpgradeTimeout`, which is set to the same derived budget and also contains its own gate time.

`repro: static`. This is arithmetic on the measured count and the upgrader's call order, not an observed failure. It needs the extreme supported link and a slow decision at the same moment.

## The change

- **Move `ADMISSION_DECISION_TIMEOUT_MS` into `link-budget.ts`**, keeping its value, its comment and its `cuts off by design` directive from the prereq. It has to live there, not be imported there: `link-budget.ts` is a leaf module, and importing the gater from it would create the cycle link-budget → membership-connection-gater → seed-bootstrap → link-budget, which can leave a top-level constant undefined at evaluation. Update the gater, the revocation enforcer, `index.ts` and `control-read-retry.spec.ts` to import it from `link-budget.ts`. No re-export from the gater (AGENTS.md: no backwards compatibility yet).
- **`relayedDialBudgetMs(rtt) = RELAYED_DIAL_ROUND_TRIPS × rtt + ADMISSION_DECISION_TIMEOUT_MS`.** That is 16 000 ms at the default. `connectionManagerTimeouts` follows automatically, so the dialer's `dialTimeout` and the listener's `inboundUpgradeTimeout` stay equal, which their doc requires. `DEFAULT_CONTROL_COHORT_PER_ADDRESS_DIAL_TIMEOUT_MS` and the peer-join push dial follow too.
- **`relayedRequestBudgetMs(rtt) = relayedDialBudgetMs(rtt) + CIRCUIT_REQUEST_ROUND_TRIPS × rtt`.** That is 23 000 ms at the default. Its dial part is a dial like any other. Today it is written as `RELAYED_REQUEST_ROUND_TRIPS × rtt`; keep `RELAYED_REQUEST_ROUND_TRIPS` as the round-trip count it documents.
- **`relayReservationBudgetMs(rtt) = RELAY_RESERVATION_ROUND_TRIPS × rtt + 2 × ADMISSION_DECISION_TIMEOUT_MS`.** That is 18 000 ms at the default. A party-run relay is a control node, so it decides the connection and then, separately, the reservation (`denyInboundRelayReservation`, same deadline). The 4-count's slack of 2 round trips covers both decisions at the default declaration, but not at a declared round trip under 2 000 ms. Counting them explicitly makes it hold at every declaration.
- **Nothing else changes.** `circuitRequestBudgetMs` (a request over an already-open connection) opens no connection, so no gate runs.

What it costs: a peer that is truly gone holds each operation longer before it is abandoned. A per-address dial goes from 14 s to 16 s, a whole control-cohort dial of one peer across its 4 addresses from 56 s to 64 s, and a wake call's two attempts from 42 s to 46 s. Record this in the `relayedDialBudgetMs` doc, the way `DECLARED_LINK_ROUND_TRIP_MS` records its own cost.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **Import cycle.** Verified by the type check and by the cadre-core suite loading at all. A constant that is `undefined` at module evaluation shows up as `NaN` budgets, and `link-budget.spec.ts` fails.
- **Proportionality spec.** `link-budget.spec.ts` ~34-36 asserts that 3× the declaration gives 3× the budget. That no longer holds. Replace it with the property that is still true: the round-trip part scales and the allowance does not (`budget(d) - budget() === count × (d - DECLARED_LINK_ROUND_TRIP_MS)`), one assertion per budget function. The default-value assertions (~27-29) gain the allowance term.
- **Per-field overrides** (`network.controlCohort.perAddressDialTimeoutMs`, `strandBackfill.dialTimeoutMs`, `DialWakeOptions.timeoutMs`) are used as given. An override replaces the whole budget, allowance included. Say so where the override is documented, if it isn't already obvious.
- **The existing NOTE on `connectionManagerTimeouts`** (a per-field override above the declaration does not raise libp2p's limits) still holds. Do not change it.
- **Does the measured 12 094 ms include a gate?** Read `relayed-dial-cost-by-latency.integration.ts`'s topology. If the dialed node was a gated control node, the measurement contains a fast decision (milliseconds) and the allowance covers the slow case. If it was not, say so. Record whichever is true in one sentence in the `link-budget.ts` module doc. Don't re-run the scenario; it is opt-in and slow.
- **Nodes that run no gate** (the dedicated relay container, open-strand nodes with the raw gater) get a budget 2 s larger than their dials need. That is harmless: a larger budget only lengthens failure on a dead peer.
- **Stale numbers in prose.** grep cadre-core `src` and `docs` for `14 000`, `14_000`, `21 000`, `21 s`, `56 s`, `42 s`, `32 s` and fix every one that quotes a budget derived from these functions. `tickets/backlog/debt-libp2p-nodes-built-outside-cadre-core-miss-the-ping-defaults.md` quotes "14 s each at the default". Update that number in place.
- **Doc section.** Update the prereq's `docs/cadre-consistency.md` row for the admission deadline, and the "checked relationships" list, from "once … lands" to the actual derivation.
