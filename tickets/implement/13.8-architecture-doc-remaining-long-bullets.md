## Form to use

**Labelled sub-paragraphs for all of them** — not new headings. Each of these sits in a list or a short section alongside siblings of the same kind (other gates, other resolution paths, other wake mechanisms), and pulling one out into a heading would break the list it is a member of. The item keeps its bold opening claim and first paragraph; each further topic becomes its own paragraph, blank-line separated, indented to the list item (two spaces under `-`, three under a numbered item such as `3.`), opening with a short bold run-in label ending in a period.

Most of these already contain bold phrases mid-sentence that mark where a new topic starts (for example `**The bring-up quiet period:**` and `**The relay-reservation seam:**` inside the connection-gate item, `**Every key entering the anchor is shape-checked first**` inside the anchor item). Use those as the split points and as the labels. Where bold is used only for emphasis inside a sentence (`**data-only**`, `**serially, in that order**`), it is not a split point.

The two plain paragraphs are not list items: split them into several paragraphs at topic changes, each with a run-in label. For the dial-budget paragraph the expected topics are: the declared value and its default; what is derived from it; how a host overrides it; what a larger value costs.

Exception: if, on reading, one of these items turns out to describe a self-contained mechanism with four or more distinct parts and no sibling items of its kind, make it a headed section as the first ticket did, leave a one-sentence item linking to it, and say which and why in the handoff.

## History

Apply the rule: present behaviour stays; a problem found and fixed becomes a slug or commit reference of at most one sentence; a past fact stays only when it is the standing reason for the design. Phrases to look for: "used to", "before … landed", "now" used only to mark recency, "landed in", "fixed upstream", dated measurements. Before dropping a passage confirm its record exists — `ls tickets/complete | grep <slug>`, `grep '"<slug>"' tickets/.pruned-tickets.jsonl`, or `git log --oneline --all -- 'tickets/**/*<slug>*'`. No findable record: the passage stays, moved to the end of its item.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **Numbered-list continuity.** The two hibernation items are entries 3 and 5 of a numbered list. A continuation paragraph indented less than three spaces ends the list and restarts numbering at 1. Check the rendered numbering (or the raw indentation) after editing.
- **Relative references.** The strand-address items refer to "the RPC above" and each other; `grep -n "next bullet\|previous bullet\|bullet above\|bullet below" docs/architecture.md` and fix any inside touched text.
- **Inbound anchors.** `#strand-address-resolution`, `#seed-delivery-protocol` and `#relay-integration` are linked from outside the document. No heading is renamed under the default form; if the exception above is used, confirm the new heading slug is unique.
- **Open-ticket references are not history.** Links into `tickets/backlog/` and `tickets/blocked/` describe open work; keep them. Nine backlog tickets cite this document by path and none by line number, so no ticket needs updating.
- **No identifier lost.** Diff the backticked tokens of each old item (`git show HEAD:docs/architecture.md`) against the new file; list each token that disappeared in the handoff with the pointer that replaced it.
- **Meaning must not change, and items between 350 and 500 words are out of scope.** Eleven further lines measure 350–500 words; leave them. Do not correct content that looks stale — record it in the handoff.
- **Concurrent edits.** Work from the file as found; touch only the listed items.
