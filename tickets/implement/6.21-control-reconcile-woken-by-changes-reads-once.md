# The control reconcile pass runs on change, with a 2-minute backstop

## What the pass is and what it costs today

`CadreNode.reconcileControlCohort` is the control network's upkeep pass. Each run refreshes the membership gate (the in-memory set of peers the per-stream gate admits), publishes this machine's own address record if it never has, refreshes relay delegate grants and running strands' address books, reaps rows a revocation retired, files the revocation ledger marker, publishes cross-party joins, then lists the party's machines and dials those it is not connected to. Passes are single-flight. Today a `setInterval` runs it every 15 s (`DEFAULT_CONTROL_COHORT_RECONCILE_MS`), plus once 1 s after start and on `self:peer:update`.

Measured (`message-cost-accounting`, cadre case, 2026-10-20, recorded in `tickets/complete/6.05-control-membership-rows-cached-until-changed.md`): with the membership rows view in place, an idle two-machine minute makes 22 control block reads and 43 `/sync` streams (48 without the view). The view serves rows for at most `MEMBERSHIP_VIEW_MAX_AGE_MS`, which equals the 15 s reconcile default. That is past the 10 s read-repair window, so every reload of a held block starts a background `/sync` to the sibling. The lever left is how often the tables are re-read at all: the pass's period and, tied to it, the view's maximum age.

## Settled design

### Two subscriptions

**Membership gate watch.** In `start()`, right after `setMembershipChangeListener` is wired, take `controlDatabase.watchTables(MEMBERSHIP_GATE_TABLES, onChange, { label: 'membership-gate' })` with no backstop, where `MEMBERSHIP_GATE_TABLES` is `CadreControl.CadrePeer`, `OwnerKey`, `Revocation`, `CadreInvite`, `CadreInviteUsage`, `OwnerGrant`, `OwnerGrantUsage` (everything the gate refresh and the anchor sync read). `onChange` awaits `refreshMembershipGate('table-change')`, then triggers a reconcile pass (`controlReconcileRuns?.trigger(0)`).

Why separate from the pass: a pass can spend a whole `controlDialBudget()` (86 s at the default declared link) dialing one unreachable sibling. With the gate refresh only inside the pass, a revocation landing mid-dial would wait for that dial. Separately, a replicated `Revocation` or `CadrePeer` change re-materializes the gate within the 50 ms coalescing window of reaching this node's storage, and `hangUpRevokedControlPeers` (the end of every gate refresh) hangs up on a removed machine then.

**Reconcile scheduler.** Replace `controlCohortReconcileTimer` with `controlReconcileRuns: TableChangeWatch | null`, built in `startRecordRefresh` by `scheduleRuns(...)` (no table subscription of its own; the gate watch triggers it) with `backstopMs` = `config.network.controlCohort.reconcileMs ?? DEFAULT_CONTROL_COHORT_RECONCILE_MS`. `stopRecordRefresh` disposes it. Its `onChange` waits for any pass already in flight, then starts a fresh one:

```ts
await this.reconcileControlCohortInFlight?.catch(() => undefined);
await this.reconcileControlCohort();
```

A scheduled run must not simply join an in-flight pass. That pass may have read its rows before the change that woke the run, so joining it would drop the change until the backstop. Explicit public calls keep today's contract: they join an in-flight pass.

### What starts a pass

- start: the eager pass after `startRecordRefresh` becomes `trigger(0)`;
- `self:peer:update`: `trigger(0)` (today it calls the pass directly);
- a change to any gate table, through the gate watch;
- a control `connection:open` or `connection:close` whose remote peer is in `authorizedControlPeers`: `trigger(0)`. On `connection:open` also call `controlDatabase.invalidateMembershipViews()` first (make it public). A machine that was offline when a sibling committed learns of the commit only when it next reads the block from storage. Dropping the views on reconnect makes that read happen now, not after the maximum age. Add both to the existing handlers in `wireControlConnectionListeners`; keep the growth-edge logic as it is.
- a redial retry and a strand-address due time, armed at the end of a pass (below);
- the backstop;
- explicit calls (`addDrone` callers, `claimNode`, `redeemCadreInvitation`, `cadre-cli start-owner`, tests), unchanged.

Only connections to authorized siblings trigger a pass, not every control connection. A connection to a relay, a bootstrap node or another party's machine changes nothing the pass acts on, except that it can satisfy the pass's "connected" check. Two scenarios rely on that not starting a pass (below). Consequence, accepted: the connected-only steps (reap, marker, joined-strand publication, first self-record publish) on a one-machine party whose only connection is a relay run at the next backstop or membership change, up to 2 minutes later than the first connection, not up to 15 s.

### Retries armed by a pass

At the end of every pass (scheduled or explicit), compute the next wake with one small pure function, `nextReconcileWakeMs`, in `control-cohort.ts`, and `trigger` it when it is defined:

- **Redial.** If the pass left a selected sibling unconnected (`dialControlSibling` returned false, including "no dialable address"), or made a cold-start bootstrap dial that failed, increment `reconcileRedialStreak` and wake after `min(CONTROL_COHORT_REDIAL_MS × 2^(streak − 1), backstop)`, with `CONTROL_COHORT_REDIAL_MS = 15_000` (today's period) in `control-cohort.ts`. A pass that left nothing unconnected resets the streak to 0. A stranded cold-start node therefore keeps trying forever, as `dialColdStartBootstrap`'s doc requires (15 s, 30 s, 60 s, then every 2 minutes). An offline sibling (a phone switched off) costs one dial every 2 minutes once the streak saturates, not one every 15 s.
- **Strand addresses.** The earliest `strandAddrAskDueAt` entry later than the pass's start time, if it is sooner than the backstop: wake at `due − now` (floor 0). The "later than the pass's start" rule is the strand watcher's (`strand-watcher.ts` `scheduleLaunchRetry`). A due time at or before the start belongs to a (sibling, strand) the pass skipped for another reason, and waking for it would spin. This keeps `STRAND_PEER_ADDR_RETRY_MS` (60 s) for a sibling that refused or did not answer.
- The smaller of the two wins; `undefined` when neither applies (the backstop covers the rest).

`runReconcileControlCohort` needs to report "left a sibling unconnected" and "a cold-start dial failed" to the wrapper. Use a private result type; keep the public `ControlCohortReconcileResult` (`{ dialed }`) unchanged. The streak is a node field, reset in `stopRecordRefresh` with the other per-session state.

Timing that rides the backstop and needs nothing new, checked: `refreshDelegateGrants` re-announces at most every `DELEGATE_GRANT_TTL_MS / 2` (15 min), so 2 minutes late is within the 30 min grant. FRET record re-merge and formation-address re-merge guard the peerStore's one-hour expiry. Self-record heartbeat has its own 7.5 min timer.

### Backstop and maximum age

- `DEFAULT_CONTROL_COHORT_RECONCILE_MS` becomes `120_000`, documented as the backstop: it bounds gate staleness for a change that reaches this node with no storage event (a machine outside the block's cohort on a party larger than `CONTROL_REPLICATION_BREADTH`). Keep the constant's and the config field's names; rewrite their docs (`control-cohort.ts`, `types.ts` `controlCohort.reconcileMs`).
- `MEMBERSHIP_VIEW_MAX_AGE_MS` stays `= DEFAULT_CONTROL_COHORT_RECONCILE_MS`, so it becomes 2 minutes with it. Update its NOTE to say the reconnect invalidation above now covers the offline-machine case, so the maximum age is left only for the outside-the-cohort case.

### "Read each table once per pass" is declined

The plan asked to take `Revocation` and `CadrePeer` once per pass and thread them through the gate refresh, reap, sibling enumeration and strand-address refresh. Since the membership rows view landed, each of those reads is an in-memory scan when the view is current. `control-founding-consult-budget.spec.ts` pins the idle pass at 0 consults. Threading one row set would add parameters to `refreshAuthorizedControlPeers` (also the local-write listener path), `resolvePeerAddrs` (public) and `connectedSiblingTargets`, and save no storage read. A pass's reads going stale during the pass is harmless: a change during the pass triggers another one. Do not restructure. Rewrite the NOTE in `runReconcileControlCohort` (~3838-3856) and the NOTE on `strandAddrRefreshTargets` (~7311) so they state the current cost (served from the view; storage only after a change or the maximum age) and drop the "share one row-set across the pass" suggestion.

The invitation and grant chain reads (the plan's item 2) are handled by the prereq `control-invitation-and-grant-chains-cached-until-changed`, which puts those tables in the view instead of caching verdicts.

### The ledger marker

`revocation-ledger-marker-removed` (implement/, waiting on `control-collections-created-at-cadre-founding`) deletes the marker's filing step. Leave the step where it is in the pass. If that ticket lands first, there is nothing to leave.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **Write lock.** `notifyMembershipChanged` runs the membership listener with the control write lock held. Neither new subscription runs consumer code there: the watch handler only arms a timer (`table-change-watch.ts`), and both `onChange`s run from that timer. Verify by inspection; no pass or gate refresh is started inline from `onInvalidate`.
- **Local membership writes refresh the gate twice:** once from the awaited membership listener (so `authorizePeer` / `removePeer` see their change before returning), once from the gate watch 50 ms later. The second one is served from the view. Accepted. Update the NOTE in `refreshAuthorizedControlPeers` (~2856-2866) about every refresh re-arming the control backfill: refreshes now also come from replicated changes.
- **Self-wake.** A pass's own writes (reap, first self-record publish, marker, joined-strand publication) change gate tables and so wake one follow-up gate refresh and one follow-up pass, which finds nothing to do. Bounded at one; no loop. A view reload that lands a read-repair with new content wakes one pass the same way. Check by inspection, and in the idle measurement (an idle minute with no membership change should show no passes beyond backstops and redials).
- **A burst of membership writes** (an owner adding several machines) folds into one gate refresh and one pass through the 50 ms window and single-flight.
- **No second concurrent dial to one sibling.** Passes stay single-flight (`reconcileControlCohortInFlight`), and a scheduled run waits for an in-flight pass before starting. A change-driven pass after a failed dial does dial that sibling again; changes are rare while idle, and a burst folds into one pass. By inspection.
- **Teardown.** `stopRecordRefresh` disposes the scheduler and `cleanup()` disposes the gate watch before `controlDatabase.close()` (the database's registry would dispose it too; `dispose` is idempotent). A run already in flight stops at the pass's existing `_running` guards. A stop then start builds both afresh.
- **Gate watch before the scheduler exists.** The gate watch is taken at start and the scheduler about 1 s later in `startRecordRefresh`. A change in between refreshes the gate and finds `controlReconcileRuns` null; the eager start pass covers it.
- **A table not yet touched** hears local commits only (`table-change-watch.ts` NOTE). The start gate refresh reads `CadrePeer`, `OwnerKey` and `Revocation`. The chain tables are touched only once some row is admitted by an invitation or grant. Until then nothing depends on them, and the first dependent `CadrePeer` / `OwnerKey` row is itself a change on a touched table. A chain load served by the committed-read path is not kept and does not arm the bridge; a chain row that arrives after that waits for the next change or the backstop. Note it at the gate watch.
- **Node waiting to be claimed** (`isAwaitingClaim`). The gate refresh and the pass behave as they do today; only the trigger changes. By inspection.
- **Cadres larger than 16 machines.** Machines outside a block's cohort see membership changes at the backstop or at the view's maximum age (both 2 minutes), not 15 s. Record as a `NOTE:` on `DEFAULT_CONTROL_COHORT_RECONCILE_MS`, like the one on `PENDING_JOIN_BACKSTOP_MS`.
- **Joined-strand publication.** A join recorded locally (`JoinedStrandSession.onLocalChange`) does not trigger a pass. It is published on the next pass while connected: any membership change, any sibling connecting, or the backstop, so at most 2 minutes. Two scenarios rely on it not being published promptly (below). Update `docs/strands.md` → "Publication" ("within one pass" becomes "on the next pass, at most the 2-minute backstop while connected") and the NOTE at `joined-strand-store.ts` ~452 ("within a reconcile interval").
- **`addDrone` / `--addr`.** The drone's `CadrePeer` insert is a gate-table change, so a pass dials the drone within about 50 ms even if the caller does not call `reconcileControlCohort()`, and a failed dial retries on the redial schedule. Rewrite `addDrone`'s doc ("otherwise the next timed reconcile pass does ... allow for one more timed pass") and `packages/cadre-cli/README.md` ~201 to match.

- `cadre-core/test/control-founding-consult-budget.spec.ts`. The node parks `reconcileMs`, but founding's own `OwnerKey` / `CadrePeer` writes will now start gate refreshes and passes on their own, which can land inside a measured phase. - `integration-tests/.../control-cohort-edge-carries-data.integration.ts` and `control-cohort-three-node-isolation.integration.ts` (`reconcileMsB: 600_000`, "the recurring timer provably never fires"). C's record reaching B now starts a pass on B. During the negative window B's dial gate denies every dial to C, so no link forms whatever starts the pass, and that assertion stands. After the gate opens, the link may come from B's redial retry or from the test's explicit pass. Assert that B holds a connection to C after the explicit pass, not that this particular pass's `dialed` lists C. Rewrite "provably never fires" comments.
- `strand-always-on-replica-hosts-cross-party-join.integration.ts` (phone parked) and `strand-moves-between-cadres.integration.ts` (N parked). Both need a recorded join to stay unpublished until the test runs a pass. Under this design neither the formation connection to another party's machine nor the local join record starts a pass. A membership change on that machine, or a new connection from one of its own party's machines, still would, as `self:peer:update` already does (both files carry a NOTE naming it). Extend those NOTEs to name both, and run both scenarios.
- Scenarios with a short `reconcileMs` (2 000 ms in `node-fixtures.ts` `PHONE_RECONCILE_MS`, `machine-removal`, `owner-anchor-follows-owner-changes`, `owner-grant-backup-recovery`, `control-cohort-auto-convergence`, `control-cohort-cold-start-retry`) now get a 2 s backstop and a redial capped at 2 s. ## Tests

- `control-founding-consult-budget.spec.ts` stays the regression guard for the idle pass's reads (above).
- One new spec, `cadre-core/test/control-cohort-wake.spec.ts`, for `nextReconcileWakeMs`, because it is real branching and its spin rule is easy to break. Cases: a streak of 1, 2, 3 and a large one with an unconnected sibling gives 15 s, 30 s, 60 s and then the backstop; no unconnected sibling and a strand-address due time 40 s after the pass start gives 40 s; a due time at or before the pass start is ignored (`undefined` when nothing else applies); when both apply the smaller wins; with a 2 s backstop the redial is capped at 2 s.
- No test for the subscription or connection wiring: it is registration, and the scenarios above exercise it.
