  get denied by its own members once their authorized-set snapshots go
  non-empty — because owner identity (a key) and node identity (a peer id)

  reviewer whether this should become a documented pattern (every
  non-relay owner participating in a full-mesh cluster needs
  `bootstrapNodes` pointed at itself on every other cluster member) rather
  than a per-scenario fix, since production topologies that mirror
  scenario 4's shape (dedicated owner, neither sender nor receiver) would
  hit the same gap outside tests.
