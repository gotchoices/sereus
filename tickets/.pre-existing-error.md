## Why it is not this ticket's

The losses happen during the trio's boot, before any code that ticket added runs: its diff is a new helper, three constants and a new case placed sixth in the file. Three earlier runs of that ticket's case alone (`-t "silent after answering"`), with the same boot, logged no `concurrent modification` at all; this was 1 boot in 4.

`[peer-reauthorize]` at boot is A's write-while-alone drain (`CadreNode.reissuePendingPeerWrites` → `ControlDatabase.reauthorizeCadrePeer`): A authorized B and C while it had no control connections, and re-touches their rows once they connect, at the moment B and C publish their own address records. That is the race `tickets/backlog/bug-owner-write-racing-a-machines-own-publish-fails` describes (an owner write to a machine's `CadrePeer` row and that machine's own publish refuse each other with `ConcurrentModificationError`, which the control-write retry does not re-run). That ticket's evidence is `machine-removal.integration.ts`; this is the same class reaching the degraded-cohort scenario's boot, from both sides: A's re-touch of B's row lost to B's publish, and C's own publish lost to another writer of C's row (not traced; A's re-touch of C's row is the likely one). No entry in `tickets/.pre-existing-known.md` covers it.
