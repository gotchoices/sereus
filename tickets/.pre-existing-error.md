## Unverified lead

The workspace resolves `@optimystic/*` through `link:` to `../optimystic`, which is at 1.8.1 and requires `p2p-fret ^1.0.0`. FRET 1.0.0 forwards signed address records in its neighbour snapshots, so a drone may now learn the other drone's address from the owner and dial it. That would explain a three-member cohort, but it was not confirmed (no run against the previous FRET, no trace of the dial). If it is the cause, the tests' assumption that a drone cannot find its sibling no longer holds, and the question is whether the cap was a requirement or an observation.

`tickets/.pre-existing-known.md` lists `harness-party-control-cohort` for a different test ("commits a real control write once the cohort really spans three machines"), which passed here.
