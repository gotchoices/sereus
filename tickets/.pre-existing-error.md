When control replication to the restarted node is slow, the loop gate's 60 s expires first. Confirm by moving the `awaitSwapOn(returned, …)` gate ahead of the loop gate, or gating the loop on it; if the loop then still never latches once the swap is on A2, that is a reconciler defect (the rebuild after a restart), not timing.
