## Why it is pre-existing

`PeersIncompleteError` does not appear anywhere in `../optimystic/packages/db-core/src` or its `dist`. The local `../optimystic` checkout is on `main` at `76239355` (2026-11-18, "tickets: garden report, runner stopped to move machines"), with a clean tree, and is behind `origin/main` (`18a5277d9`, 2026-11-24). The export landed upstream after that (`git log --all -S PeersIncompleteError` in `../optimystic` finds it only on `origin/main`), and sereus's `control-write-retry.ts` has used it since `cd9e4978e` (2026-11-21). The stale-build guard does not catch this, because the sibling's `dist` is up to date with its own (old) `src`. The fix is outside this repo: update the `../optimystic` checkout to `origin/main` and rebuild it, which the sibling-repo rule leaves to its owner.
