# Upstream: a write made alone while other members are known owes no one a copy

**Blocked on:** a dependency outside this repo. Unblocks when Optimystic releases a fix for `../optimystic/tickets/fix/1-a-write-made-alone-while-other-members-are-known-never-reaches-them` and Sereus adopts that release.

## The defect

GitHub issue gotchoices/sereus#44. When a node's cohort for a block resolves to only itself, `CoordinatorRepo.commitSolo` commits with `quorum: 'local'` and records the block in the under-replication ledger with `missingPeerIds: []`. The drain therefore has nobody to push to, on reconnect or after a restart. A joiner that wrote alone and then stopped never delivers the write; the commit reported success.

Optimystic's ticket names the likely fix (record as owed the members the node knows serve the network but is not connected to) and keeps the refuse-or-wait policy for one- and two-machine groups out of scope, owned by its partition-healing design. Optimystic's agent has been told; it is ranked first on their board.

## What Sereus does when it lands

- Adopt the release (floors in `packages/cadre-core/package.json` and the other `@optimystic/*` dependents; the adoption pattern is in the recent `adopt optimystic …` commits).
  Sereus master already calls the plugin's `onCommitDurability`, `onCommitFullyHeld` and `txnBridge.dispose()` (`strand-commit-durability-reaches-the-app`), which published 1.12.1 lacks, so the raised floors must be a release that carries them, and Sereus must not be published before that.
- Re-run `joiner-first-commit-over-slow-relay.integration.ts` and read the `unconfirmed [...]` field its sixth arm logs for the founder's write made while the joiner is stopped: the fix is in when it names the stopped joiner's strand peer as owed. The fourth and fifth arms log the same field for the joiner's lone commit (made with the founder's strand hibernated), a second place to read it.
- If the fix stage of `joiner-write-made-alone-lost-when-it-stops` found a Sereus-side delivery defect after restart, confirm both together.
- Reply on #44 and close it.

## Sereus-side findings (fix stage of `joiner-write-made-alone-lost-when-it-stops`, 2026-10-11)

- The scenario is committed; its third arm reproduced in the one committed-scenario run here and in all four scratch runs of the same shape. In this harness the solo commit has a Sereus cause too: a deferred join tore down the joiner's one connection to the founder and rebuilt over a store that already held everything (`implement/strand-deferred-join-keeps-its-runtime`). That fix may flip the third arm before this upstream fix lands. If it does, the arm no longer tests this ticket; the upstream fix is then confirmed by its own Optimystic repros, not by this scenario.
- Delivery after a restart is not blocked on this ticket in Sereus: peer-join backfill pushes the restarted joiner's whole store when its strand node reconnects, and the founder accepted the solo-committed blocks (they carry the self-signed solo proof). The scenario's fourth arm measured 24.5 s from the restart to the founder holding the rows. On a slow link the strand nodes never reconnect at all; that is `implement/strand-peers-redial-on-the-declared-link-budget` and `blocked/fret-maintenance-ping-dial-budget-below-a-relayed-dial`, not Optimystic.
- (2026-10-11, implement stage of `strand-deferred-join-keeps-its-runtime`) The deferred join now keeps its runtime and retries only the database build, but the third arm still commits alone, so it stays `it.fails` and still exercises this ticket. In this harness the founder-to-joiner circuit is cut by the dedicated relay's stock libp2p ping monitor about 2 s after the founder's catch-up push, at the moment the joiner's schema read fails; the retry 5 s later builds the database from the delivered blocks while the joiner is connected only to the relay. Details on `backlog/debt-libp2p-nodes-built-outside-cadre-core-miss-the-ping-defaults` (third arm). The joiner was not writable while connected to the founder in any run, so the "connection dropped between writable and the write" case was not observed.
- (2026-10-12, implement stage of `strand-commit-durability-reaches-the-app`, linked Optimystic RC `1b352916`) The third arm still fails, so it stays `it.fails`. One run reproduced the documented shape (joiner writable after 128.9 s with only the relay connected, seat committed alone, rows never reached the founder); the other never became writable within its 600 s first-sync budget. The scenario's new sixth arm, where the FOUNDER writes after its strand node dropped the stopped joiner's connection, saw the RC report `local`, `confirmed 1 / cohort 1`, `unconfirmed []`: the stopped member was not named as owed. The commit was still followed (`recordedBy` the founder) and reported fully held 7.8 s after the joiner restarted.
- Strand nodes stay without a persistent `kvStore`: the under-replication ledger is redundant with backfill's push on connect. A `NOTE:` in `peer-join-backfill.ts` says to add one before that module is narrowed or deleted.
- (2026-10-17, implement stage of `slow-link-restart-arms-lost-their-lone-commit`, linked Optimystic 1.15.0 at `28e6e044`) The third arm no longer reproduces this defect: since `strand-founder-commits-every-table-collection` the joiner becomes writable while connected to the founder, so its first commit goes to both members and fails at the commit (`complete/optimystic-ping-handler-timeout-below-the-monitor-deadline`) instead of being made alone. In the redesigned fourth and fifth arms, the joiner's lone commit named the hibernated founder as owed in 4 of 4 passing runs (`local`, 1 of 2, `unconfirmed [<founder strand peer>]`, followed). The sixth arm's founder write still reported `local`, 1 of 1, `unconfirmed []` (followed, and `strand:commit-held` came 38.1 s after the joiner restarted; 1 run). Optimystic's `a-write-made-alone-names-the-members-it-is-owed-to` is in its `complete/` and tagged from v1.13.0, and Sereus's floors are already `^1.15.0`, so a human should decide whether the sixth arm's `unconfirmed []` is a remaining gap upstream or whether this ticket can close.

- (2026-10-20, `adopt-optimystic-1-17-0`, Optimystic 1.17.0 linked at `0e70f551`) Nothing in Optimystic 1.16.0 or 1.17.0 changes what a lone commit records as owed.
