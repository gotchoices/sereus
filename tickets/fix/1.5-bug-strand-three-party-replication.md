# Three-party strand formation does not replicate to all members

## Why this is filed now

Until tonight every strand and control convergence scenario failed for one shared upstream
replication cause (`control-db-convergence-optimystic-p2p`, fixed across `../optimystic`
`50af693` → `559df6a`). The integration suite went from ~18 failures to 3. This is one of the
three left underneath, and it is the only remaining *strand* failure — the two-party strand
scenarios in the same file now pass:

- `should form strand, start instances, and replicate data` — **passes**
- `should support multiple independent strands between same parties` — **passes**
- `should form a strand with three parties` — **fails**

```
strand-formation-e2e.integration.ts:668
  Error: Timeout waiting for data replicates to Bob after 15000ms
```

Two parties replicate; three do not. That, plus the fact that everything else in the file is
green, is the whole shape of the evidence.

## First thing to check, because it is cheap and likely

The replication cluster size was changed tonight from a hardcoded 3 to a value derived from the
party, with a **default and floor of 2** — see `bug-cluster-size-exceeds-cadre-size` in
`complete/` and `packages/quereus-plugin-sereus/src/cluster-size.ts`. That change was made for
the control network, where a party's cadre is usually one or two nodes.

A three-party strand is a different population: its members are the *parties in the strand*, not
one party's nodes. Check what `clusterSize` a three-party strand instance actually resolves to,
and whether a cohort of three running with a size of 2 explains a member being left out. Verify
before acting — a plausible story is exactly what has been wrong twice on this failure already —
but it is the first thing to measure.

Relevant: upstream, `clusterSize` is used both as the replication factor *and* as the yardstick a
member uses to judge whether an inbound write comes from a legitimately-sized group. That
conflation is raised upstream as `clustersize-conflates-replication-factor-and-admission-yardstick`
in `../optimystic/tickets/plan/`. If a three-member cohort is running with a size of 2, the
mismatch cuts in the opposite direction from the control-network case and may not be benign.

## Other angles if that is not it

- **Is it always Bob**, or whichever party joined last / third? Run it a few times — if the victim
  is positional, it is a formation-order bug; if it is random, it is a timing or quorum bug.
- **Does the third party ever get admitted** to the strand, or does it join and then never receive?
  These look identical from a `waitUntil` timeout and need different fixes.
- With three members, quorum arithmetic stops being degenerate — two of three is a real majority
  where two of two was unanimity. Several of tonight's upstream fixes changed behavior specifically
  at small cohort sizes; three is the first size where the general path runs. Check the upstream
  `corroboratorCapacity` computation against a three-member cohort.
