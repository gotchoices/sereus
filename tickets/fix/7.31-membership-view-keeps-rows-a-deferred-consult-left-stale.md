# A reconnecting machine's membership views can stay stale after its reload's consults are deferred

## What happens

A machine that was offline while a sibling committed catches up on the membership tables through the reconnect reload: `CadreNode.armMembershipViewReload` drops every membership view 11 s (`MEMBERSHIP_VIEW_RECONNECT_RELOAD_MS`) after a sibling connects and starts a reconcile pass, whose reads go to storage, where each block read past Optimystic's 10 s read-repair window starts a cohort consult (`CoordinatorRepo.startHeldBlockConsult`). A consult that lands a newer revision raises a table change, which drops that table's view again.

Optimystic runs at most 16 such consults at once (`MAX_HELD_BLOCK_CONSULTS`). A read past that cap is served from the local copy, starts nothing, and leaves the block unstamped "so a later read starts it". But the membership view installs the rows that read returned and answers every later read from memory, and a table whose consults were all deferred raises no change. So no later storage read comes until the view's maximum age (`MEMBERSHIP_VIEW_MAX_AGE_MS`, 2 minutes), and the machine's membership snapshot misses the rows for that long.

## Measured

`cadre-invite-any-member.integration.ts`: owner A mints a grant and an invitation and stops; device P joins at member M (M writes P's `OwnerKey`, `CadrePeer`, `CadreInvite` and `CadreInviteUsage` rows); A restarts and must list P within 60 s.

- With the code as it is, A lists P about 12.27 s after reconnecting (3 of 3 runs on 2026-10-20 after `new-machine-self-record-written-once`). In a passing debug run, A acquired 8 newer blocks (`cluster-tx:read-repair-applied`) *before* the 11 s reload, and the reload triggered 22 consults with none deferred.
- With P's publish after joining removed (the change `implement/invited-machine-skips-its-first-publish` makes), A never listed P in 5 of 5 runs, each timing out at 60 s; in 3 of them A's second self-write was removed too. A sixth run of the same code passed because P happened to write again (its boot timer found its record not current). To reproduce, make `publishFirstSelfRecord` and the boot timer in `scheduleSelfRegistration` call `publishSelfRecordUnlessCurrent()` unconditionally, with the redemption's record remembered before the dial as that ticket describes. In a failing debug run (`DEBUG='sereus:cadre:node,optimystic:*'`), A acquired nothing before the reload (45 consults, all `read-repair-noop`). At the reload it attempted 38: 23 started, 15 were `cluster-tx:read-repair-deferred { inFlight: 16 }`, including every consult of `default/cadrecontrol/CadreInvite` and `default/cadrecontrol/CadreInviteUsage`. A derived P's owner key (the `OwnerKey` and grant chain arrived) but never listed P as a member, whose admission chain runs through those two tables.
- Why the writes hide it: on A, a second self-write after reconnecting conflicts with M's newer revision (`ConcurrentModificationError`, the "known benign race" at `scheduleSelfRegistration`), and P's own publish after joining adds a concurrent write at M. Their retries pull newer blocks early, so fewer consults are in flight at the reload. Which of the two writes is load-bearing was not isolated: the scenario passed whenever P wrote again after joining and failed whenever it did not.

Not verified: that A lists P once the 2-minute maximum age passes (the scenario gives up at 60 s).

## Expected

A machine that reconnects reads the membership rows its siblings committed while it was away within the reconnect reload's bound (about 12 s at the defaults), whether or not any of its own writes happen to conflict, and however many of its blocks are stale at once. A deferred consult must be retried by some later storage read, rather than waiting on a view whose rows nothing invalidates.

Constraints:

- No new cohort traffic in the common case: a reconnect where nothing changed should cost what it costs today (the message-cost accounting's `cadre` and `invite` cases are the yardstick; record before and after).
- Optimystic is a sibling repo and read only here. If the right fix needs Optimystic to report that a read's consult was deferred, that is an upstream request, and the cadre-side workaround and its cost go in this ticket.
- Once this lands, the NOTEs in `scheduleSelfRegistration` and on `publishFirstSelfRecord` can go, and `implement/invited-machine-skips-its-first-publish` can run. Update `docs/architecture.md` → "After a sibling connects", which names this gap.
