# Release notes — pending

- **A phone re-opening a shared strand over a slow relayed link is no longer told, wrongly, that no other member was reachable.** A machine that had been away can take longer to get the strand's data back than one joining for the first time: on a measured relayed link a re-attach took up to about 80 s to become writable, one outside report of a re-attach took about 150 s, and a real phone's first join through the public relay took 178 s, both past the old 120 s wait. `addStrand` now waits up to **300 s** by default (`strandFirstSync.timeoutMs` to change it). The cost: when no other member is reachable at all, `addStrand` takes five minutes rather than two to report it. The error, `StrandAwaitingFirstSyncError`, now says the data has not arrived yet (no member reachable, or a slow link still syncing) and is still retryable; to keep waiting past it, call `whenStrandWritable(strandId)` or listen for `strand:writable`.
