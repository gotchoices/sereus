# Release notes — pending

- **Closed strands created on `@serfab/*` 0.13.0 or earlier must be recreated after upgrading.** Their founding manager key is derived from the read secret every member holds, so any member can act as the founder, and this cannot be repaired in place. The founder launch now refuses such a strand with `PreSplitStrandIdentityError` (exported from `@serfab/cadre-core`), and join attempts against it are rejected with `'Host strand must be recreated'` (`HOST_STRAND_MUST_BE_RECREATED_REASON`) instead of being told to retry. Unpublish the strand and found a new one.
