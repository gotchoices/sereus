News

Releases, apps and milestones across Sereus and its stack, newest first. Detailed notes for every Sereus release are on GitHub.

2026

ReleaseNov 2026

Sereus 1.23: quieter connected cohorts

When two or three machines in a cohort stay connected to each other, reading unchanged data no longer makes them ask each other for anything newer every 10 seconds. The release moves to Optimystic 1.27 and Quereus 5.4.

AppsNov 2026

Chat sends photos, and Bonum runs on several devices

Sereus Chat attachments now carry their bytes through a chunked blob store, and WebRTC connections were verified directly from phone to phone. In Bonum, a second device and then a web browser joined the same cadre, set up with QR-code invitations.

StackNov 2026

Quereus 5.0

The SQL engine behind every strand reached its fifth major version, about a year and a half after its first commit. Sereus 1.22 adopted it within days.

AppsNov 2026

WyattERP begins

Design work started on WyattERP, a parametric manufacturing-control system for companies that build modular products. It will run on Sereus, with Bonum handling the accounting.

AppsOct 2026

Taleus runs on strands, with an Android build

Taleus, the reboot of MyCHIPs on Sereus, now stores each tally in a two-party strand. An Android testing build went up on sereus.org/taleus, along with invite links that open the app directly.

ReleaseOct 2026

A month of cadre features: offline invitations, node claiming, change notifications

In 1.10, a request to join can wait for an inviter who is offline, retrying in the background for up to 30 days. In 1.11, the relay answers STUN itself, so the separate TURN servers were removed. In 1.12, apps can subscribe to change notifications on strand tables. In 1.14, a new always-on node waits to be claimed by its owner's phone, which scans the node's code as a QR code. In 1.18, the control store migrates in place when a machine upgrades.

ReleaseSep 2026

Built for phones

In 1.2, a two-party insert over a relay takes about half as long as before (4.5–5.9 s, down from 8.9–10.2 s), because each write needs fewer network round trips. 1.3 and 1.4 stopped slower phones from being disconnected while they sync, with native encryption and a more patient connection check. 1.6 added @serfab/cadre-rn, a shared kit for React Native apps that run a cadre node. In 1.7, two parties that can reach each other only through a relay keep replicating after both restart.

MilestoneSep 18, 2026

Sereus 1.0 and Optimystic 1.0

Sereus reached 1.0 on the same day as Optimystic 1.0, the distributed storage and transaction layer beneath it. The week before, 0.13 had made multi-machine setups work: two phones each reachable only through a relay can form a strand and replicate both ways, a strand can span two parties with two machines each, and a machine added later catches up on the data it missed.

AppsSep 2026

Taleus gets its own page

sereus.org/taleus went up, introducing tallies, pledges and lifts, with invite links ready for the app.

EcosystemAug 2026

VoteTorrent publishes downloadable apps

VoteTorrent, the transparent-elections project that Optimystic grew out of, updated its website with downloadable Android builds.

AppsJul 2026

Sereus Health launches at sereus.org/health

Sereus Health became the first Sereus app with its own page and an Android download: a private tracker for finding what makes you feel better or worse, with starter catalogs to import. Sereus Chat's page followed, so that invite links open the app. The same month, Chat added relay mode, and Bonum ran its books on a Sereus backend for the first time.

AppsMay 2026

Sereus Chat stores its first messages in a strand

Chat moved from a design prototype to the real stack: conversations are now kept in Sereus strands.

ReleaseApr 2026

First tagged Sereus release

Sereus 0.2.0 started regular versioned releases of the @serfab/* packages, along with documentation of the polyfills each runtime needs. Forty-two more followed by mid-November.

StackMar 2026

Quereus 1.0

The Quereus SQL query processor reached 1.0, giving strands a stable SQL engine with declarative constraints.

StackFeb 2026

Sereus packages on npm, and on React Native

The first @serfab packages were published to npm, with React Native support. Sereus Health was the first app to load the stack.

StackJan 2026

Relays, Docker images and Fret

Nodes connected through a relay for the first time, and Docker images arrived for running relays and always-on nodes, which became the basis for keeping phones reachable. Fret, the distributed hash table, was split out of Optimystic into its own package.

2025

AppsNov – Dec 2025

Chat, Health and Bonum begin

Three apps started within a month of each other: Sereus Chat (November 11), Sereus Health (November 27) and Bonum, for personal and business finance (December 6). Each began from stories and specifications in a design-first workflow.

StackNov 2025

Strand formation comes to Sereus

The bootstrap protocol that forms a strand between two parties, first proven in Taleus, was ported into Sereus with its tests passing.

MilestoneOct 28, 2025

sereus.org goes live

The Sereus website launched, describing consent-based strands and the use cases they could enable.

StackAug 2025

First strand formation test passes

In the Taleus repository, the first test of the tally bootstrap passed. That handshake, in which two parties invite each other into a shared database, became the model for how all strands form.

StackJun 2025

Optimystic becomes its own project

The distributed B-tree and transaction layer written for VoteTorrent moved into its own repository as Optimystic.

StackApr – May 2025

Where it started: Quereus and Taleus

Work began on Quereus, the SQL processor (April), and on Taleus, the effort to rebuild MyCHIPs credit on a peer-to-peer foundation (May). Both became foundations of Sereus: Quereus as its SQL engine, and Taleus as the origin of its strand-forming protocol.
