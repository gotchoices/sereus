Apps Built on Sereus

The use cases describe what the fabric makes possible. These are applications that are actually being built on it, each running its own cadre of devices and keeping its shared data in strands. Some of them can be installed today.

The Android builds below are testing builds, downloaded from each app's page and installed directly. They are not in the Play Store yet.

Available to try

Sereus Chat

Android testing build

Private messaging, with no accounts and no servers.

Chat with people you trust, by invitation only. There is no phone number, no sign-up and no company in the middle. Accepting an invitation forms a private strand between the two of you, and messages travel peer to peer, through a relay you choose when needed.

Sereus Health

Android testing build

Discover what makes you feel better, or worse.

A private personal health tracker. Log what you do, what is happening around you and how you feel, then graph items together to find the patterns. Your data stays on your own devices. Syncing and sharing with people you trust, through Sereus, is in progress.

Learn more and download →

Taleus

Android testing build

Private credit between people you trust.

Keep a tally with someone you trust: a shared ledger that only the two of you hold, recorded in a two-party strand. Value moves as signed pledges, and lifts clear balances around loops of tallies. Taleus is a reboot of MyCHIPs on Sereus.

Sereus Bonum

In development

Personal and business finance, built on decentralized trust.

Double-entry bookkeeping with a balance-sheet focus: separate books for each business unit, reconciliation, multiple currencies, receivables and payables. It supports collaboration and oversight with the people you trust, such as a partner or an accountant.

W

WyattERP

In design

Parametric manufacturing control, built on Sereus.

Product definition, inventory, purchasing and production for companies that build modular, configurable products. Accounting is handled by Bonum. Its first user is a startup that makes modular hydroponic growing systems.

Build your own

Each of these apps is a Sereus app (sApp): an ordinary application, plus a SQL schema that defines the tables its strands share. The same stack is available to you.
